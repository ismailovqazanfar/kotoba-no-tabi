雲 (on: ウン; kun: くも; bulud / cloud) | origin: Rain (雨) on top, 云 below – an old picture of a curling cloud. A cloud. | old story: What brings rain: 雲 (kumo), cloud.
波 (on: ハ; kun: なみ; dalğa / wave) | origin: Water (氵) on the left, skin (皮; sound) on the right: the rising and falling skin of the water – a wave. | old story: The skin of the water: 波 (nami), wave; 電波 (denpa), radio wave.
机 (on: キ; kun: つくえ; masa / desk) | origin: The meaning part is wood (木) and the sound part is 几 (a small table on legs). A table made of wood: a desk. | old story: Wood + a table on legs: 机 (tsukue), desk.
千 (on: セン; kun: ち; min / thousand) | origin: A person (人) with a line across it. To write "thousand", 人, which sounded alike, was taken and joined with a "one" mark. | old story: Person + one line: 千 (sen), thousand. 千円 (sen'en), 1,000 yen.
金 (on: キン; kun: かね; qızıl, pul / gold, money) | origin: Its origin is not entirely clear. One explanation sees pieces of metal in the earth, or a casting mould, under a lid (今, which gives the sound). Meaning: metal, gold, money. | old story: Metal in the earth: お金 (okane), money; 金曜日 (kin'youbi), Friday.
晩 (on: バン; kun: –; axşam / evening) | origin: The meaning part is the sun (日) and the sound part is 免. The time when the sun has gone, late in the day: evening. | old story: The sun has gone: 晩 (ban), evening; 今晩 (konban), this evening.
昼 (on: チュウ; kun: ひる; günorta, gündüz / noon, daytime) | origin: The old form is 晝: the sun (日) with its limits drawn by a brush (聿). The time when the sun is in the sky: daytime, noon. | old story: Sun time: 昼 (hiru), daytime; 昼ご飯 (hirugohan), lunch.
昨 (on: サク; kun: –; dünən, keçən / yesterday, last) | origin: The meaning part is the sun, day (日) and the sound part is 乍. The day that has passed: yesterday, the previous. | old story: The day gone by: 昨日 (kinou), yesterday; 昨年 (sakunen), last year.
試 (on: シ; kun: ため・す; sınamaq / try, test) | origin: The meaning part is words (言) and the sound part is 式 (rule). Testing by words against a rule: to try, an exam. | old story: Test with words: 試験 (shiken), exam; 試す (tamesu), to try.
水 (on: スイ; kun: みず; su / water) | origin: A picture of flowing water: the main stream in the middle and splashing drops at the sides. On the left it becomes 氵: 海, 洗, 泳. | old story: Stream and drops: 水 (mizu), water. 水曜日 (suiyoubi), Wednesday.
