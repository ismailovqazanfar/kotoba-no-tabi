趣 (on: シュ; kun: おもむき; zövq, maraq / taste, interest) | origin: The meaning part is to run (走) and the sound part is 取. Hurrying towards something: interest, taste. | old story: Where the heart runs: 趣味 (shumi), hobby.
記 (on: キ; kun: しる・す; qeyd etmək / record) | origin: The meaning part is words (言) and the sound part is 己. Writing words down: to record. | old story: Write the words down: 日記 (nikki), diary; 記念 (kinen), commemoration.
祈 (on: キ; kun: いの・る; dua etmək / pray) | origin: The meaning part is 示 (altar, the sacred) and the sound part is 斤. To pray, to wish. | old story: Wish from the heart: 祈る (inoru), to pray, to wish.
馬 (on: バ; kun: うま; at / horse) | origin: A picture of a horse: head and mane on top, and the four dots at the bottom are legs and tail. | old story: A maned horse: 馬 (uma), horse; 競馬 (keiba), horse racing.
原 (on: ゲン; kun: はら; çöl, mənbə / field, origin) | origin: A spring (泉) coming out at the foot of a cliff (厂). Where water begins: source; later a plain. | old story: The source of water: 原因 (gen'in), cause; 野原 (nohara), field.
掃 (on: ソウ; kun: は・く; süpürmək / sweep) | origin: A hand (扌) and a broom (帚). To sweep with a broom. | old story: A hand with a broom: 掃除 (souji), cleaning; 掃く (haku), to sweep.
除 (on: ジョ; kun: のぞ・く; kənar etmək / remove) | origin: The meaning part is steps, hill (阝) and the sound part is 余. Palace steps; later "to remove". | old story: Remove: 掃除 (souji), cleaning; 除く (nozoku), to exclude.
眠 (on: ミン; kun: ねむ・る; yatmaq / sleep) | origin: The meaning part is eye (目) and the sound part is 民. With eyes closed: to sleep. | old story: Closing eyes: 眠い (nemui), sleepy; 眠る (nemuru), to sleep.
弱 (on: ジャク; kun: よわ・い; zəif / weak) | origin: Two decorated bows. Bows made for show, useless in battle: weak. | old story: A decorative bow: 弱い (yowai), weak.
練 (on: レン; kun: ね・る; məşq etmək / practice) | origin: The meaning part is silk (糸) and the sound part is 柬 (to select). Boiling silk to soften it: to refine; later to practise. | old story: Soften the silk: 練習 (renshuu), practice.
