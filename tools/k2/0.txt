夜 (on: ヤ; kun: よ、よる; gecə / night) | origin: The evening moon (夕) under a person's arm: the time that begins when the moon rises – night. | old story: The moon is up: 夜 (yoru), night; 今夜 (kon'ya), tonight.
決 (on: ケツ; kun: き、きめる、きまる; qərara almaq / decide) | origin: Water (氵) on the left, to break open (夬) on the right: breaking a dyke to let the water through. From this came "to decide". | old story: Open the dyke, decide: 決めます (kimemasu), to decide.
実 (on: ジツ; kun: み、みの、みのる、みのり; həqiqi; meyvə / real, fruit, nut) | origin: The old form 實: a house (宀) with strings of coins inside. A full house – full, real; later also the fruit of a tree. | old story: A full house: 実は (jitsu wa), actually; 実 (mi), fruit.
森 (on: シン; kun: もり; meşə / forest) | origin: Three trees (木) together, one above and two below. In Chinese characters three of the same sign mean "a lot", so 森 is a thick, dense forest. | old story: One tree 木, two trees 林, three trees 森 (mori), a dense forest.
申 (on: シン; kun: もう、もうす、もうし; ərz etmək (təvazökar) / report, speak humbly) | origin: A picture of lightning branching across the sky. Later "to state"; in Japanese 申します, "my name is..." (humble). | old story: A humble word: 申します (moushimasu), I am called...
島 (on: トウ; kun: しま; ada / island) | origin: A bird (鳥) and a mountain (山): a mountain in the sea where birds land – an island. | old story: A mountain with birds: 島 (shima), island; 島国 (shimaguni), island nation.
苦 (on: ク; kun: くる、にが、くるしい、くるしむ、くるしめる、にがい、にがる; əzab, acı / suffering, bitter, painful) | origin: Grass (艹) on top, old (古; sound) below: a bitter-tasting herb – bitter, hardship. | old story: A bitter herb: 苦い (nigai), bitter; 苦しい (kurushii), painful, hard.
具 (on: グ; kun: -; alət, vasitə / implement, tool) | origin: A precious vessel (like 目) above two hands holding it: a thing held in the hands – a tool, equipment. | old story: A thing in the hands: 道具 (dougu), tool; 家具 (kagu), furniture.
息 (on: ソク; kun: いき; nəfəs / breath) | origin: A nose (自) above a heart (心): the breath that comes through the nose from the chest. | old story: Breath: 息 (iki), breath; 息子 (musuko), son.
商 (on: ショウ; kun: あきな、あきなう、あきない; ticarət / trade, deal) | origin: It was the name of the ancient Shang (商) state. A popular explanation says its people were known as traders, so it came to mean "trade". | old story: A people of traders: 商品 (shouhin), goods; 商店 (shouten), shop.
