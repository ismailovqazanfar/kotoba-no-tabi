触 (on: ショク; kun: さわ・る; toxunmaq / touch) | origin: A horn (角) and an insect (虫). An insect touching with its antennae: to touch. | old story: Touch with antennae: 触る (sawaru), to touch.
渡 (on: ト; kun: わた・る; keçmək / cross) | origin: The meaning part is water (氵) and the sound part is 度. Crossing to the other side of water: to cross; to hand over. | old story: Cross the water: 渡る (wataru), to cross; 渡す (watasu), to hand over.
寂 (on: ジャク; kun: さび・しい; tənha / lonely) | origin: The sound part 叔 under a roof (宀). A quiet, silent house: lonely, sad. | old story: A silent house: 寂しい (sabishii), lonely.
湯 (on: トウ; kun: ゆ; isti su / hot water) | origin: The meaning part is water (氵) and the sound part is 昜 (sun, warmth). Hot water. | old story: Sun-warmed water: お湯 (oyu), hot water; 銭湯 (sentou), public bath.
点 (on: テン; kun: –; nöqtə / point) | origin: The old form is 點: the meaning part black (黑) and the sound part 占. A black spot: a dot; later "a point, a mark". | old story: A black spot: 点 (ten), dot, point; 点数 (tensuu), score.
駐 (on: チュウ; kun: –; dayanmaq / stay, park) | origin: The meaning part is horse (馬) and the sound part is 主 (to stay in one place). A horse coming to a halt: to stop, to stay. | old story: Halt the horse: 駐車場 (chuushajou), car park.
徳 (on: トク; kun: –; fəzilət / virtue) | origin: Road (彳), straight (直) and heart (心). Walking the road with a straight heart: virtue. | old story: A straight heart: 道徳 (doutoku), morals; 聖徳太子 (Shoutoku Taishi).
太 (on: タイ; kun: ふと・い; qalın, böyük / thick, big) | origin: Big (大) with a dot beneath: bigger than big. Thick, fat; "the greatest, chief". | old story: Bigger than big: 太い (futoi), thick; 太陽 (taiyou), the sun.
法 (on: ホウ; kun: –; qanun, üsul / law, method) | origin: The meaning part is water (氵) with 去. The old form also had a mythical animal of justice (廌). A rule that flows as level as water: law, method. | old story: Level as water: 方法 (houhou), method; 法律 (houritsu), law.
隆 (on: リュウ; kun: –; çiçəklənən / prosperous) | origin: The meaning part is hill (阝) with parts rising up (生) on the right. Rising high: elevation, prosperity. | old story: A rising hill: 法隆寺 (Houryuuji), Horyu-ji temple in Nara.
