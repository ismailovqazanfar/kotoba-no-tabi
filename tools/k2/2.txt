働 (on: ドウ; kun: はたら・く; işləmək / work) | origin: A kanji created in Japan (kokuji). A person (亻) on the left and movement (動) on the right. A person on the move: to work. | old story: Person + movement = work: 働く (hataraku), to work.
験 (on: ケン; kun: –; sınaq / test, effect) | origin: The meaning part is horse (馬) and the sound part is 僉. It first meant checking a horse to judge its quality: a test, an experience. | old story: Check the horse: 試験 (shiken), exam; 経験 (keiken), experience.
船 (on: セン; kun: ふね; gəmi / ship) | origin: The meaning part is boat (舟) and the sound part is 㕣. A large boat: a ship. | old story: A big boat: 船 (fune), ship; 船便 (funabin), sea mail.
飛 (on: ヒ; kun: と・ぶ; uçmaq / fly) | origin: A picture of a bird flying with its wings spread: head and neck at the top, wings and feathers at the sides. | old story: Spread wings: 飛ぶ (tobu), to fly; 飛行機 (hikouki), aeroplane.
友 (on: ユウ; kun: とも; dost / friend) | origin: Two right hands (ナ and 又) reaching for each other: two people joining hands, friends. | old story: Hand in hand: 友達 (tomodachi), friend; 友人 (yuujin), friend (formal).
誕 (on: タン; kun: –; doğum / birth) | origin: The meaning part is words (言) and the sound part is 延 (to stretch). It first meant "to stretch the truth, to exaggerate"; later it was used in 誕生, birth. | old story: Birth day: 誕生日 (tanjoubi), birthday; 誕生 (tanjou), birth.
普 (on: フ; kun: –; ümumi / universal) | origin: 並 (side by side) on top and the sun (日) below. Sunlight spreading evenly everywhere: general, ordinary. | old story: Light falling everywhere: 普通 (futsuu), ordinary; local train.
肉 (on: ニク; kun: –; ət / meat) | origin: A picture of a slice of meat with muscle fibres inside. On the left of other kanji it becomes ⺼, which looks like 月: 腹, 脱. | old story: Meat with fibres: 肉 (niku), meat; 牛肉 (gyuuniku), beef.
買 (on: バイ; kun: か・う; almaq / buy) | origin: A net (网, now 罒) on top and shell money (貝) below. Netting goods with money: to buy. | old story: Cast a net of money: 買う (kau), to buy; 買い物 (kaimono), shopping.
紙 (on: シ; kun: かみ; kağız / paper) | origin: The meaning part is fibre, thread (糸) and the sound part is 氏. A thin sheet made of fibres: paper. | old story: A sheet of fibres: 紙 (kami), paper; 手紙 (tegami), letter.
