返 (on: ヘン; kun: かえ・す; qaytarmaq / return (sth)) | origin: The meaning part is road (辶) and the sound part is 反 (to turn back). Turning back on the road: to return. | old story: Turn back: 返す (kaesu), to give back; 返事 (henji), reply.
心 (on: シン; kun: こころ; ürək, qəlb / heart, mind) | origin: A picture of the heart. The ancient Chinese believed thought and feeling lived in the heart: heart, mind. On the left it becomes 忄. | old story: Heart and mind: 心 (kokoro), heart; 心配 (shinpai), worry.
健 (on: ケン; kun: すこ・やか; sağlam / healthy) | origin: The meaning part is person (亻) and the sound part is 建 (to build). A well-built person: healthy, strong. | old story: A sturdy person: 健康 (kenkou), health.
康 (on: コウ; kun: –; rahatlıq, sağlıq / peace, health) | origin: Its origin is debated; one explanation sees grain husks. It means "peaceful, at ease, healthy". | old story: At ease, healthy: 健康 (kenkou), health.
保 (on: ホ; kun: たも・つ; qorumaq, saxlamaq / protect, keep) | origin: A person (亻) carrying a child on the back (呆). Looking after a child: to protect, to keep. | old story: Protect the child: 保険 (hoken), insurance; 保存 (hozon), saving.
険 (on: ケン; kun: けわ・しい; sıldırım, riskli / steep, risky) | origin: The meaning part is hill (阝) and the sound part is 僉. A steep hill: dangerous. | old story: Steep: 危険 (kiken), danger; 保険 (hoken), insurance.
薬 (on: ヤク; kun: くすり; dərman / medicine) | origin: Grass (艹) on top and 楽 (ease, giving the sound) below. A herb that brings relief: medicine. | old story: A herb that eases: 薬 (kusuri), medicine; 薬局 (yakkyoku), pharmacy.
邪 (on: ジャ; kun: –; pis, zərərli / evil, wicked) | origin: On the right 阝 (a town), on the left the sound part 牙. It means "crooked, bad". In 風邪 it is a "bad wind", a cold. | old story: A bad wind: 風邪 (kaze), cold; 邪魔 (jama), getting in the way.
味 (on: ミ; kun: あじ; dad / taste) | origin: The meaning part is mouth (口) and the sound part is 未. What the mouth senses: taste. | old story: In the mouth: 味 (aji), taste; 意味 (imi), meaning; 趣味 (shumi), hobby.
弾 (on: ダン; kun: ひ・く; çalmaq, güllə / play (instrument), bullet) | origin: The meaning part is bow (弓) and the sound part is 単. A bow that shoots pellets: a bullet; pulling and releasing a string: to play (a string instrument). | old story: Pull and release the string: 弾く (hiku), to play (piano, guitar).
