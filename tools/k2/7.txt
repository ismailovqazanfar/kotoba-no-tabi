重 (on: ジュウ; kun: おも・い; ağır / heavy) | origin: A person (人, at the top) carrying a heavy sack (like 東) on the ground (土). Heavy, important; also "layer". | old story: A heavy sack: 重い (omoi), heavy; 重要 (juuyou), important.
季 (on: キ; kun: –; mövsüm / season) | origin: Grain (禾) on top and a child (子) below. Young grain. The growing stages of grain mark the parts of the year: season. | old story: Young grain: 季節 (kisetsu), season.
海 (on: カイ; kun: うみ; dəniz / sea) | origin: The meaning part is water (氵) and the sound part is 毎. Great water: the sea. | old story: Great water: 海 (umi), sea; 海外 (kaigai), overseas.
界 (on: カイ; kun: –; dünya, sərhəd / world, boundary) | origin: The meaning part is field (田) and the sound part is 介 (to be between). The boundary between fields: a border, a world. | old story: The border between fields: 世界 (sekai), the world.
寿 (on: ジュ; kun: ことぶき; uzun ömür / long life) | origin: The old form is 壽: an old man of long life and a sound part. Long life, congratulations. In 寿司 it is written for its sound. | old story: Long life: 寿命 (jumyou), lifespan; 寿司 (sushi), sushi.
司 (on: シ; kun: –; idarə etmək / administer) | origin: Its origin is debated: it is explained as someone giving orders with hand and mouth. To administer, to manage. In 寿司 it is used for its sound. | old story: The one in charge: 司会 (shikai), master of ceremonies; 寿司 (sushi), sushi.
豚 (on: トン; kun: ぶた; donuz / pig) | origin: Meat (月 = 肉) and pig (豕). A pig raised for its meat. | old story: Meat + pig: 豚 (buta), pig; 豚肉 (butaniku), pork.
待 (on: タイ; kun: ま・つ; gözləmək / wait) | origin: The meaning part is road (彳) and the sound part is 寺. Standing on the road and waiting. | old story: Wait on the road: 待つ (matsu), to wait; 招待 (shoutai), invitation.
泳 (on: エイ; kun: およ・ぐ; üzmək / swim) | origin: The meaning part is water (氵) and the sound part is 永 (a long current). Moving forward through water: to swim. | old story: Forward through water: 泳ぐ (oyogu), to swim; 水泳 (suiei), swimming.
欲 (on: ヨク; kun: ほ・しい; istək / desire) | origin: The sound part is 谷 and the meaning part a person with an open mouth (欠). Opening your mouth and craving something: desire. | old story: Open your mouth and want: 欲しい (hoshii), to want (a thing).
