若 (on: ジャク; kun: わか・い; gənc / young) | origin: Its origin is debated: it is explained as a kneeling woman combing her hair or gathering herbs. Today it means "young". | old story: Youth: 若い (wakai), young; 若者 (wakamono), young people.
短 (on: タン; kun: みじか・い; qısa / short) | origin: An arrow (矢) and the sound part 豆. In old times short distances were measured with an arrow: short. | old story: An arrow's length: 短い (mijikai), short; 短所 (tansho), weak point.
頭 (on: トウ、ズ; kun: あたま; baş / head) | origin: The meaning part is head (頁) and the sound part is 豆. Head. | old story: Head: 頭 (atama), head; 頭痛 (zutsuu), headache.
髪 (on: ハツ; kun: かみ; saç / hair) | origin: Long hair (髟) on top and the sound part below. Hair. | old story: Long strands: 髪 (kami), hair; 髪型 (kamigata), hairstyle.
顔 (on: ガン; kun: かお; üz / face) | origin: The meaning part is head (頁) and the sound part is 彦. The front of the head: the face. | old story: The front of the head: 顔 (kao), face; 笑顔 (egao), smile.
耳 (on: ジ; kun: みみ; qulaq / ear) | origin: A picture of an ear: its rim and the folds inside. | old story: Ear: 耳 (mimi), ear.
鼻 (on: ビ; kun: はな; burun / nose) | origin: The nose (自) on top and the sound part 畀 below. After 自 came to mean "self", this kanji was made for the nose. | old story: Nose + sound: 鼻 (hana), nose; 鼻水 (hanamizu), runny nose.
背 (on: ハイ; kun: せ; kürək, boy / back, height) | origin: The meaning part is body (月 = 肉) and the sound part is 北 (two people standing back to back). The back. | old story: Back to back: 背中 (senaka), back; 背 (se), height.
緑 (on: リョク; kun: みどり; yaşıl / green) | origin: The meaning part is silk thread (糸) and the sound part is 彔. Silk dyed green: green. | old story: Green silk: 緑 (midori), green; 緑茶 (ryokucha), green tea.
額 (on: ガク; kun: ひたい; məbləğ, alın / amount, forehead) | origin: The meaning part is head (頁) and the sound part is 客. The forehead; later a frame (a hung tablet) and a sum of money. | old story: Forehead, sum: 額 (hitai), forehead; 金額 (kingaku), amount of money.
