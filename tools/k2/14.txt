才 (on: サイ; kun: –; istedad, yaş / talent, age) | origin: It is explained as a shoot just breaking through the soil. Inborn ability: talent. In Japanese it is also written instead of 歳 to count years of age. | old story: A new shoot: 天才 (tensai), genius; 5才 (gosai), five years old.
久 (on: キュウ; kun: ひさ・しい; uzun müddət / long time) | origin: A bent person held from behind by something. Staying put a long time: long, for a long time. | old story: For a long time: 久しぶり (hisashiburi), long time no see.
牧 (on: ボク; kun: まき; otlaq, pastor / pasture, pastor) | origin: An ox (牛) and a hand holding a stick (攵). Herding cattle with a stick: pasture, herding. | old story: Herd with a stick: 牧場 (bokujou), ranch, pasture.
被 (on: ヒ; kun: かぶ・る; başa qoymaq, məruz qalmaq / put on (head), suffer) | origin: The meaning part is clothing (衤) and the sound part is 皮 (skin). Covering the body with a garment: to cover; later "to suffer". | old story: Cover the body: 被る (kaburu), to put on (a hat); 被害 (higai), damage.
掛 (on: カ; kun: か・ける; asmaq / hang) | origin: The meaning part is hand (扌) and the sound part is 卦. Hanging something up by hand. | old story: Hang by hand: 掛ける (kakeru), to hang; to put on (glasses).
帽 (on: ボウ; kun: –; papaq / hat) | origin: The meaning part is cloth (巾) and the sound part is 冒 (to cover the head). Cloth worn on the head: a hat. | old story: Cloth on the head: 帽子 (boushi), hat.
眼 (on: ガン; kun: め; göz / eye) | origin: The meaning part is eye (目) and the sound part is 艮. Eye, eyeball. | old story: Eye: 眼鏡 (megane), glasses; 眼科 (ganka), ophthalmology.
鏡 (on: キョウ; kun: かがみ; güzgü / mirror) | origin: The meaning part is metal (金) and the sound part is 竟. Ancient mirrors were made of polished metal: mirror. | old story: Polished metal: 鏡 (kagami), mirror; 眼鏡 (megane), glasses.
探 (on: タン; kun: さが・す; axtarmaq / search) | origin: The meaning part is hand (扌) and the sound part is 罙 (deep). Reaching deep with the hand to search. | old story: Reach in deep: 探す (sagasu), to look for.
団 (on: ダン; kun: –; qrup / group) | origin: The old form is 團: 專 (spinning, round) inside a frame (囗). A round mass, a ball: a group. | old story: A round mass: 布団 (futon), futon; 団体 (dantai), group.
