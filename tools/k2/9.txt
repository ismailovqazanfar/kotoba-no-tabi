思 (on: シ; kun: おも・う; düşünmək / think) | origin: On top was originally the skull, the brain (囟), which later turned into 田; below is the heart (心). With head and heart: to think. | old story: Head and heart: 思う (omou), to think; 思い出 (omoide), memory.
橋 (on: キョウ; kun: はし; körpü / bridge) | origin: The meaning part is wood (木) and the sound part is 喬 (tall, arched). Built of wood and arching over: a bridge. | old story: A wooden arch: 橋 (hashi), bridge.
置 (on: チ; kun: お・く; qoymaq / put, place) | origin: A net (罒) on top and the sound part 直 (straight) below. Setting a net straight in one spot: to put, to place. | old story: Set the net in place: 置く (oku), to put; 位置 (ichi), position.
刻 (on: コク; kun: きざ・む; oymaq, vaxt / carve, time) | origin: The meaning part is knife (刂) and the sound part is 亥. To carve or notch with a knife. Old clocks marked time with notches, so it also means "moment, time". | old story: Notch with a knife: 時刻 (jikoku), time; 遅刻 (chikoku), lateness.
済 (on: サイ; kun: す・む; bitmək, həll olunmaq / finish, settle) | origin: The meaning part is water (氵) and the sound part is 斉 (even). Crossing a river to the other bank: to finish, to settle. | old story: Cross the river: 済む (sumu), to be over; 経済 (keizai), economy.
独 (on: ドク; kun: ひと・り; tək / alone) | origin: The old form is 獨: the meaning part dog (犭) and the sound part 蜀. The traditional explanation: dogs do not stay together but roam apart: alone. | old story: Roaming alone: 独身 (dokushin), single; 独り (hitori), alone.
皆 (on: カイ; kun: みな; hamı / everyone) | origin: 比 (two people side by side) on top and 白 (to say) below. Words spoken by everyone together: all, everybody. | old story: Everyone together: 皆さん (minasan), everyone.
引 (on: イン; kun: ひ・く; dartmaq / pull) | origin: A bow (弓) and its string (丨). Drawing the bowstring: to pull. | old story: Draw the string: 引く (hiku), to pull; 引っ越す (hikkosu), to move house.
暗 (on: アン; kun: くら・い; qaranlıq / dark) | origin: The meaning part is the sun (日) and the sound part is 音. Without the sun: dark. | old story: Without sun: 暗い (kurai), dark; 暗記 (anki), memorisation.
目 (on: モク; kun: め; göz / eye) | origin: A picture of an eye, turned upright, with the pupil inside. | old story: Big eyes: 目 (me), eye; 目的 (mokuteki), purpose.
