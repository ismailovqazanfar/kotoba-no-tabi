隣 (on: リン; kun: となり; qonşu, yanında / neighbour, next to) | origin: The meaning part is mound, settlement (阝) and the sound part is 粦. Villages lying side by side: neighbouring. | old story: Villages side by side: 隣 (tonari), next door.
封 (on: フウ; kun: –; möhürləmək / seal) | origin: Mounds of earth (圭) on the left and a hand (寸) on the right. Piling up earth and planting trees on a boundary: to enclose, to seal. | old story: Close and seal: 封筒 (fuutou), envelope.
筒 (on: トウ; kun: つつ; boru / tube) | origin: The meaning part is bamboo (⺮) and the sound part is 同. A hollow bamboo stem: a tube, a case. | old story: A hollow bamboo: 筒 (tsutsu), tube; 封筒 (fuutou), envelope.
祭 (on: サイ; kun: まつ・り; bayram, festival / festival) | origin: Meat (月 = 肉), a hand (又) and an altar (示). Offering meat by hand: a rite, a festival. | old story: An offering by hand: 祭り (matsuri), festival.
遊 (on: ユウ; kun: あそ・ぶ; oynamaq / play) | origin: The meaning part is road (辶); the right side is a banner streaming in the wind (斿). Roaming freely like a banner: to wander, to play. | old story: Free as a banner: 遊ぶ (asobu), to play, to have fun.
簡 (on: カン; kun: –; sadə / simple) | origin: The meaning part is bamboo (⺮) and the sound part is 間. In ancient times these were bamboo slips used for writing. Short notes gave the meaning "simple, brief". | old story: A short note on a bamboo slip: 簡単 (kantan), easy, simple.
単 (on: タン; kun: –; tək, sadə / single, simple) | origin: The old form is 單. Its origin is debated: it is explained as a two-pronged hunting weapon. From very early on it meant "single, simple". | old story: Single: 単語 (tango), word; 簡単 (kantan), easy.
遠 (on: エン; kun: とお・い; uzaq / far) | origin: The meaning part is road (辶) and the sound part is 袁. A long road: far. | old story: A long road: 遠い (tooi), far; 遠足 (ensoku), school outing.
多 (on: タ; kun: おお・い; çox / many) | origin: Two pieces of meat stacked on top of each other (they look like 夕). Piled up: many. | old story: Piled up: 多い (ooi), many; 多分 (tabun), probably.
涼 (on: リョウ; kun: すず・しい; sərin / cool) | origin: The meaning part is water (氵) and the sound part is 京. The coolness that comes off water: cool. | old story: Coolness from water: 涼しい (suzushii), cool.
