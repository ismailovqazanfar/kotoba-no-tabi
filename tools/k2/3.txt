果 (on: カ; kun: は・たす; meyvə, nəticə / fruit, result) | origin: A ripe fruit (the circle that looks like 田) on top of a tree (木). Fruit; also the "fruit" of an effort, the result. | old story: Fruit on a tree: 果物 (kudamono), fruit; 結果 (kekka), result.
緒 (on: ショ、チョ; kun: お; başlanğıc, ip / beginning, cord) | origin: The meaning part is thread (糸) and the sound part is 者. The end of a thread: a beginning, a cord. In Japanese 一緒 means "together". | old story: Two ends of one thread: 一緒に (issho ni), together.
荷 (on: カ; kun: に; yük, baqaj / load, luggage) | origin: Grass (艹) on top and 何 below. 何 was first a person carrying a load on the shoulder; the meaning "load" moved to this kanji: load, luggage. | old story: The load on the shoulder: 荷物 (nimotsu), luggage, parcel.
白 (on: ハク; kun: しろ・い; ağ / white) | origin: Its origin is debated: it has been explained as an acorn, a thumbnail or a candle flame. From very early on it meant "white, bright, clear". | old story: White and clear: 白い (shiroi), white; 面白い (omoshiroi), interesting.
黒 (on: コク; kun: くろ・い; qara / black) | origin: The old form is 黑: a soot-covered chimney or window on top and fire (灬) below. The place blackened by smoke from the hearth: black. | old story: Soot from the fire: 黒い (kuroi), black; 黒板 (kokuban), blackboard.
仕 (on: シ; kun: つか・える; xidmət etmək / serve) | origin: A person (亻) on the left and 士 (a man, a retainer, also giving the sound) on the right. A person who serves another: to serve, to work. | old story: A person who serves: 仕事 (shigoto), work; 仕方 (shikata), way of doing.
良 (on: リョウ; kun: よ・い; yaxşı / good) | origin: Its origin is debated: one explanation sees a sieve for grain, another a long corridor. From very early on it meant "good". | old story: Sifted, chosen = good: 良い (yoi), good; 奈良 (Nara).
侍 (on: ジ; kun: さむらい; samuray / samurai) | origin: The meaning part is person (亻) and the sound part is 寺. Someone who waits on a superior: an attendant. In Japan the word became the name of the warrior class: samurai. | old story: One who serves a lord: 侍 (samurai), samurai.
少 (on: ショウ; kun: すく・ない、すこ・し; az / few, little) | origin: Small (小) with a slanting stroke below. Something small becoming even less: few, a little. | old story: Less than small: 少し (sukoshi), a little; 少ない (sukunai), few.
上 (on: ジョウ; kun: うえ、あ・がる; üst, yuxarı / above, up) | origin: A mark placed above a horizontal line: "above". 下 is its opposite. | old story: Above the line: 上 (ue), above; 上手 (jouzu), skilful.
