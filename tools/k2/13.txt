相 (on: ソウ; kun: あい; qarşılıqlı / mutual) | origin: A tree (木) and an eye (目). Looking closely at a tree: to examine, mutual; later "minister". | old story: Look at the tree: 相撲 (sumou), sumo; 相談 (soudan), consultation.
撲 (on: ボク; kun: –; vurmaq (sumo) / strike (sumo)) | origin: The meaning part is hand (扌) and the sound part is 菐. To strike or shove with the hand. | old story: Shove with the hand: 相撲 (sumou), sumo wrestling.
北 (on: ホク; kun: きた; şimal / north) | origin: Two people standing back to back: the back. The ancient Chinese sat facing south, so their backs were to the north: north. | old story: The back side: 北 (kita), north; 北海道 (Hokkaidou), Hokkaido.
斎 (on: サイ; kun: –; təmizlənmə (Hokusai) / purification (Hokusai)) | origin: The old form is 齋: 示 (the sacred) and 齊 (giving the sound). Purifying oneself before a rite; later a quiet study. It appears in the name of the artist Hokusai. | old story: A quiet room: 書斎 (shosai), study; 北斎 (Hokusai), the artist.
言 (on: ゲン; kun: い・う; demək / say) | origin: A mouth (口) with lines showing words coming out. To speak, word. On the left of other kanji it marks meanings to do with speech. | old story: Words from the mouth: 言う (iu), to say; 言葉 (kotoba), word.
僕 (on: ボク; kun: –; mən (kişi) / I (male)) | origin: The meaning part is person (亻) and the sound part is 菐. It first meant "servant". In Japanese it is a modest "I" used by men and boys. | old story: A modest "I": 僕 (boku), I (male speech).
負 (on: フ; kun: ま・ける; uduzmaq / lose) | origin: A person (⺈) on top and money (貝) below. A person carrying money or a burden: to bear, to owe; later "to lose". | old story: Carrying a load: 負ける (makeru), to lose; 勝負 (shoubu), contest.
不 (on: フ; kun: –; qeyri-, -sız / not, un-) | origin: Its origin is debated: it is explained as the calyx of a flower or a root. It was borrowed for its sound as a negative: "not, un-". | old story: Negation: 不便 (fuben), inconvenient; 不安 (fuan), anxiety.
放 (on: ホウ; kun: はな・す; buraxmaq / release) | origin: The meaning part is striking (攵) and the sound part is 方. Driving away: to release, to let go; to broadcast. | old story: Let it go: 放す (hanasu), to release; 放送 (housou), broadcast.
夢 (on: ム; kun: ゆめ; yuxu, arzu / dream) | origin: An eye and brows on top and night (夕) below. What closed eyes see at night: a dream. | old story: What the eye sees at night: 夢 (yume), dream.
