広 (on: コウ; kun: ひろ・い; geniş / wide) | origin: The old form is 廣: a building (广) and 黄 (giving the sound). A large hall without walls: wide. | old story: A hall without walls: 広い (hiroi), wide; 広場 (hiroba), square.
狭 (on: キョウ; kun: せま・い; dar / narrow) | origin: On the left 犭 (here it replaced a "hill" sign of the old form), on the right 夾 (squeezed between two things, giving the sound). Squeezed: narrow. | old story: Squeezed between: 狭い (semai), narrow.
入 (on: ニュウ; kun: はい・る、い・れる; girmək / enter) | origin: A point going in: two strokes meeting at a tip. To enter. | old story: A point going in: 入る (hairu), to enter; 入口 (iriguchi), entrance.
持 (on: ジ; kun: も・つ; tutmaq / hold) | origin: The meaning part is hand (扌) and the sound part is 寺. Holding in the hand: to hold, to have. | old story: Hold in the hand: 持つ (motsu), to hold; 気持ち (kimochi), feeling.
住 (on: ジュウ; kun: す・む; yaşamaq / live, reside) | origin: The meaning part is person (亻) and the sound part is 主 (a lamp standing in one place). A person staying in one place: to live. | old story: Stay in one place: 住む (sumu), to live; 住所 (juusho), address.
開 (on: カイ; kun: あ・ける、ひら・く; açmaq / open) | origin: A gate (門) with two hands inside lifting the bar (开). Removing the bar to open the gate. | old story: Lift the bar: 開ける (akeru), to open; 開く (aku), to open (by itself).
呼 (on: コ; kun: よ・ぶ; çağırmaq / call) | origin: The meaning part is mouth (口) and the sound part is 乎 (breath). Letting breath out of the mouth, calling out: to call. | old story: Call out: 呼ぶ (yobu), to call; 呼吸 (kokyuu), breathing.
塩 (on: エン; kun: しお; duz / salt) | origin: The old form is 鹽: a salt field (鹵), a dish (皿) and a watching eye (臣). Salt. | old story: Salt in a dish: 塩 (shio), salt; 塩辛い (shiokarai), salty.
砂 (on: サ; kun: すな; qum / sand) | origin: Stone (石) and few, small (少). Tiny stones: sand. | old story: Tiny stones: 砂 (suna), sand; 砂糖 (satou), sugar.
歯 (on: シ; kun: は; diş / tooth) | origin: The old form is 齒: teeth lined up in a mouth with the sound part 止 on top. Tooth. | old story: Teeth in the mouth: 歯 (ha), tooth; 歯医者 (haisha), dentist.
