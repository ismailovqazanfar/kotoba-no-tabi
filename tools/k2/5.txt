近 (on: キン; kun: ちか・い; yaxın / near) | origin: The meaning part is road (辶) and the sound part is 斤. A short road: near. | old story: A short road: 近い (chikai), near; 近所 (kinjo), neighbourhood.
男 (on: ダン; kun: おとこ; kişi / man) | origin: A field (田) on top and strength (力, a plough) below. The one who puts strength into the field: man. | old story: Strength in the field: 男 (otoko), man; 男の子 (otoko no ko), boy.
右 (on: ウ、ユウ; kun: みぎ; sağ / right) | origin: A right hand (ナ) and a mouth (口). The hand that brings food to the mouth: right. | old story: The eating hand: 右 (migi), right; 右手 (migite), right hand.
京 (on: キョウ; kun: –; paytaxt / capital) | origin: A tall building raised on a mound. The place of great buildings: the capital. | old story: A building on a hill: 東京 (Toukyou), Tokyo; 京都 (Kyouto), Kyoto.
犬 (on: ケン; kun: いぬ; it / dog) | origin: A dog drawn from the side: body, legs, tail. The dot at the top right is the ear. On the left of a kanji it becomes 犭: 猫. | old story: A dog with an ear: 犬 (inu), dog; 子犬 (koinu), puppy.
象 (on: ゾウ、ショウ; kun: –; fil, obraz / elephant, image) | origin: An elephant drawn upright: trunk and head on top, body in the middle, legs and tail below. Later it also meant "image, appearance". | old story: The animal with a trunk: 象 (zou), elephant; 印象 (inshou), impression.
箱 (on: ソウ; kun: はこ; qutu / box) | origin: The meaning part is bamboo (⺮) and the sound part is 相. A box woven from bamboo. | old story: A bamboo box: 箱 (hako), box; ゴミ箱 (gomibako), rubbish bin.
蔵 (on: ゾウ; kun: くら; anbar / storehouse) | origin: Grass (艹) on top with the sound part 臧 (to hide) beneath. Hiding things under grass: a storehouse, to store. | old story: A hidden store: 冷蔵庫 (reizouko), refrigerator, "cold storehouse".
棚 (on: ホウ; kun: たな; rəf / shelf) | origin: The meaning part is wood (木) and the sound part is 朋 (two things side by side). Wooden boards laid side by side: a shelf. | old story: A wooden shelf: 棚 (tana), shelf; 本棚 (hondana), bookshelf.
左 (on: サ; kun: ひだり; sol / left) | origin: A left hand (ナ) and a tool (工). The hand that holds the tool: left. | old story: The tool hand: 左 (hidari), left; 左手 (hidarite), left hand.
