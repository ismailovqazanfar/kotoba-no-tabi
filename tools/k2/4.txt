歌 (on: カ; kun: うた、うた・う; mahnı, oxumaq / song, sing) | origin: The meaning part is a person with an open mouth (欠) and the sound part is 哥. Sound coming out loud from the mouth: to sing. | old story: Open your mouth and sing: 歌 (uta), song; 歌う (utau), to sing.
字 (on: ジ; kun: –; hərf, işarə / letter, character) | origin: A child (子) under a roof (宀): a baby is born and the family grows. Because new characters are "born" from simple ones, it came to mean "letter, written character". | old story: Born in the house: 字 (ji), character; 漢字 (kanji), kanji.
全 (on: ゼン; kun: まった・く; bütün / whole, all) | origin: Putting in (入) on top and jade (王 = 玉) below. Keeping a flawless piece of jade: whole, complete. | old story: Flawless stone: 全部 (zenbu), all; 安全 (anzen), safe.
音 (on: オン; kun: おと; səs / sound) | origin: A line added inside the mouth of the sign for words (言): sound coming from the mouth, a tone. | old story: Sound from the mouth: 音 (oto), sound; 音楽 (ongaku), music.
内 (on: ナイ; kun: うち; daxil / inside) | origin: A house entrance (冂) and entering (入). Going in: inside, interior. | old story: In through the door: 内 (uchi), inside; 案内 (annai), guidance.
残 (on: ザン; kun: のこ・る; qalmaq / remain) | origin: On the left 歹 (remains of bones), on the right the sound part 戋. What is left behind: to remain, leftovers. | old story: What is left: 残る (nokoru), to remain; 残念 (zannen), a pity.
伎 (on: キ; kun: –; məharət (kabuki) / skill (kabuki)) | origin: The meaning part is person (亻) and the sound part is 支. A skilled performer, an actor. | old story: A master of the stage: 歌舞伎 (kabuki), "song, dance, skill".
漢 (on: カン; kun: –; Çin (Han) / China (Han)) | origin: The meaning part is water (氵) and the right side gives the sound. It was first the name of the Han River, then of the Han dynasty, and then simply "Chinese". | old story: From the Han River to China: 漢字 (kanji), Chinese characters.
妻 (on: サイ; kun: つま; arvad, həyat yoldaşı / wife) | origin: A hand (彐) arranging a woman's (女) hair. A married woman with her hair put up: wife. | old story: A woman with her hair put up: 妻 (tsuma), (my) wife.
供 (on: キョウ; kun: とも、そな・える; yoldaş, təqdim etmək / companion, offer) | origin: The meaning part is person (亻) and the sound part is 共 (offering with two hands). To present respectfully, to accompany. In 子供 it forms the word "child". | old story: Offered in two hands: 子供 (kodomo), child; お供 (otomo), accompanying.
