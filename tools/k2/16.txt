直 (on: チョク; kun: なお・す; düzəltmək, düz / fix, straight) | origin: An eye (目) with a straight line above it. Looking straight: straight, direct; later "to fix". | old story: Look straight: 直す (naosu), to fix; 直接 (chokusetsu), directly.
紹 (on: ショウ; kun: –; tanıtmaq / introduce) | origin: The meaning part is thread (糸) and the sound part is 召 (to call). Joining people like joining threads: to introduce. | old story: Join the threads: 紹介 (shoukai), introduction.
説 (on: セツ; kun: と・く; izah etmək / explain) | origin: The meaning part is words (言) and the sound part is 兌. To explain in words. | old story: Explain in words: 説明 (setsumei), explanation; 小説 (shousetsu), novel.
準 (on: ジュン; kun: –; meyar, hazırlamaq / standard, prepare) | origin: The meaning part is water (氵) and the sound part is 隼. Level as a water surface: a standard. | old story: Level as water: 準備 (junbi), preparation.
越 (on: エツ; kun: こ・す; keçmək, köçmək / cross over, move) | origin: The meaning part is to run (走) and the sound part is 戉 (an axe). To run over and beyond: to cross over. | old story: Run over it: 越える (koeru), to cross; 引っ越す (hikkosu), to move house.
他 (on: タ; kun: ほか; başqa / other) | origin: The meaning part is person (亻) and the sound part is 也. Another person: other. | old story: Another person: 他 (hoka), other; 他人 (tanin), stranger.
考 (on: コウ; kun: かんが・える; fikirləşmək / think, consider) | origin: An old man with long hair (耂) on top and a bent staff (丂) below. The deep thinking of an experienced elder: to think. | old story: An elder's thought: 考える (kangaeru), to think.
舎 (on: シャ; kun: –; ev, bina / cottage, building) | origin: A building with a roof on a foundation. A lodging, a hostel, a building. | old story: A roofed building: 田舎 (inaka), countryside; 校舎 (kousha), school building.
億 (on: オク; kun: –; yüz milyon / hundred million) | origin: The meaning part is person (亻) and the sound part is 意. A hundred million. | old story: A hundred million: 一億 (ichioku), 100,000,000.
暇 (on: カ; kun: ひま; boş vaxt / free time) | origin: The meaning part is day (日) and the sound part is 叚 (to borrow). Time "borrowed" from the day: free time. | old story: Time borrowed from the day: 暇 (hima), free time.
