Title: The Best Place

On Sunday morning, Mina returned from a trip with her family. They had spent two nights at a hotel near the sea. The hotel was lovely, but their room was not very quiet.

In the afternoon, her grandmother came to visit. “How was your trip?” she asked. “It was fun. But the seawater was cold,” Mina answered.

Her father took some photos out of his bag. “Which was better, the hotel or home?” her grandmother asked. “The hotel was better. The breakfast was delicious,” her father answered.

Her mother chose a photo of the sea. “It was cooler by the sea than in town. The airport was close to the hotel too, which was convenient.” But Mina did not choose a single photo.

“Which place did you like best on the trip?” her grandmother asked Mina. Mina thought for a moment. “A small place. It was smaller than the hotel, but I didn’t feel lonely there.”

Her father showed her a photo of a bus. “Was it this big bus? Or a small one?” Mina shook her head. “It wasn’t a bus. It didn’t cost any money either.”

Mina pointed to her mother’s red umbrella. It had rained on the second day, and all three had stood under it. “That was the best place. Dad and Mum were both closer to me than usual.”

Her father and mother both sat down beside Mina. “There’s no umbrella today, but you’re still close together,” her grandmother said with a smile. Outside, the sky was cloudy, but Mina’s heart felt warmer than spring.
