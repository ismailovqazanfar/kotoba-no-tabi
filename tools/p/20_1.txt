Title: The Summer Mountain and the Blue Light

At the beginning of summer vacation, three friends gathered in a small town. Their names were Haru, Sora, and Mio. Every summer, the three friends visited different places. This year, Haru heard a story about a blue light that could be seen at the top of a mountain.

Haru said to his friends, “Hey, want to go to that mountain? I want to see the blue light.” Sora asked, “Yeah, let's go! But do you know the way?” Mio took out an old map and said, “I'm checking the route now. We can go this way.” The three friends took water and rice balls and set off.

There were big trees and beautiful flowers along the mountain path. Sora took pictures of insects and listened to birds singing. Haru was running, but Mio was walking slowly. “Haru, you're too fast! Wait a little!” Mio called. The three friends rested under a big tree.

Around noon, the three friends reached an old bridge. But one of the wooden planks was broken. Sora asked, “This bridge is dangerous. Which way should we go?” Haru said, “I'll fix it!” But he had no tools. Mio said, “There's a small path over there.”

The three friends followed the small path and found an old shrine. A little robot was sitting in front of it. The robot's eyes glowed blue, but its leg was broken. Haru asked, “What are you doing here?” The robot said in a small voice, “I want to go home, but I can't walk.”

Sora took some small tools out of his bag. “I'm a little good at repairing machines,” he said. Haru held the robot while Mio examined its leg carefully. The three friends thought of different ways to help and repaired the robot's leg together. The robot stood up and said, “Thank you! You're all so kind.”

In the evening, the robot guided the three friends to the top of the mountain. There was an old house where many little robots lived. All their eyes glowed blue, making the mountaintop beautiful like a sky full of stars. Mio said, “So you were the blue light all along!” The robots danced happily.

Before they left, the robots gave the three friends little blue stones. “Take these and come visit us again,” they said. As the friends walked down the mountain, they talked about their day. Sora laughed and said, “Today was fun, but I'm a little tired.” Haru looked at his blue stone and said, “Let's all come back next year!” Inside the bag, the stone glowed softly.
