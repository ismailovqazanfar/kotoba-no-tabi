Title: The City in the Sky and the Starlit Path

One day, a boy named Haru found an old watch. There was a small picture of a star on its back. When Haru turned the watch, a blue light appeared. When he opened his eyes, he was in a mysterious city floating in the sky.

The city had tall buildings and golden bridges. Fish swam through the sky, and little robots walked along the roads. Haru was surprised and asked, “Where am I?” But nobody answered. Being alone in an unfamiliar city made him feel a little lonely.

Haru searched for a way home. When he reached a large intersection, the traffic light turned red. When it turned green, Haru crossed the road. Then, when he turned right at the corner, he found a shop shaped like a clock. A white cat sat in front of it.

The cat asked, “Do you want to go home?” When Haru answered, “Yes,” the cat took out a small map. “If you walk straight along this road, you'll find the Star Bridge. But when you cross it, don't look down,” the cat said. Haru thanked the cat and ran off.

When Haru reached the bridge, a large robot was standing in front of it. The robot was broken and making strange noises. When Haru approached, the robot said, “Help me.” Haru touched a small button, but nothing changed.

Haru remembered the star on the back of his watch. He took out the watch and turned the little knob on its side. Light shone from the watch, and the robot's body began to glow. The robot recovered and said, “Thank you!” Then it lit up the dark path across the bridge.

Haru crossed the bridge. As he walked without looking down, he saw a blue door at the far end. When he pulled the door open, a warm wind blew. Haru stepped inside. Suddenly, he was back in his own room.

When Haru woke up the next morning, the watch had become an ordinary watch. “Was it a dream?” he wondered. But a tiny golden fish was floating above his desk. Swimming through the air, the fish said, “Come again!” Haru laughed and said, “Next time, I'll bring a map!”
