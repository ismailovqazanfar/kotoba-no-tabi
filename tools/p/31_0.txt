Title: The Fox Wedding and the Sun-Shower

Long ago, there was a small village near a mountain. A girl named Hana lived in that village. Hana had heard from her grandmother that the mountain foxes apparently held weddings just like humans. But nobody had ever seen one of their weddings.

One spring day, Hana went to the mountain to pick flowers. The sky was blue, and the sun was shining brightly. Suddenly, however, it began to rain. “How strange! It's raining even though the sun is shining,” Hana thought.

Just then, a soft sound came from deep in the forest. It seemed that someone was ringing a little bell. Between the trees, Hana saw a fox wearing a white kimono and another wearing a black kimono. The two foxes looked very happy.

Many foxes were walking in a line behind the pair. Some carried red umbrellas, and others held flowers. “This must be a fox wedding!” Hana thought. It seemed her grandmother's story was true after all.

Hana hid behind a tree and watched quietly. Then a little fox came up to her. “Everyone might be surprised if they see a human here,” said the fox. “I'm sorry,” Hana replied, and started to leave.

But the fox in the white kimono called Hana back. “Today is a happy occasion. Would you like some sweets too?” she asked. On a red plate were round sweets that looked delicious. When Hana ate one, it tasted sweet like a flower.

Before long, the rain stopped, and a great rainbow appeared over the forest. The foxes walked beneath it and disappeared deep into the mountain. Hana hurried back to the village and told her grandmother everything. Her grandmother smiled and said, “They say foxes get married when rain falls in the sunshine.”

The next morning, a little box was waiting outside Hana's house. Inside were a red umbrella and a fox-shaped sweet. “The foxes from yesterday must have brought these,” Hana thought. But as she was about to eat the sweet, she thought she heard a tiny voice whisper, “Come again!”
