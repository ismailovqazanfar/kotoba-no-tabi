Title: The Moon Shop and the Little Umbrella

One rainy day, Mina found a little shop. It was called the Moon Shop, and there was a blue clock in its window. Mina's umbrella had a hole in it, so she went inside.

A white-haired woman was in front of a desk. “Is this a clock or a camera?” Mina asked. “A clock. But this clock shows dreams instead of the time,” the woman answered.

Next, Mina pointed to a red book. “Is that an English dictionary?” she asked. “No, it isn't. This is a cat dictionary,” the woman answered, and the book meowed.

At the back of the shop was a little yellow umbrella. “Is that for sale too?” Mina asked. “Yes, it is. That is a cloud umbrella,” the woman answered, but Mina did not quite understand.

Mina bought the umbrella and opened it outside. Suddenly, a little cloud appeared underneath it. Rain dripped from the cloud, soaking Mina's head and bag.

Mina went back into the shop. “Is this my umbrella or the cloud's?” she asked. “The cloud's. It's the little one's home,” the woman answered. “I see,” Mina said, looking at the little cloud.

At home, Mina's mother was busy watering the flowers. Mina opened the umbrella in the garden and told the cloud, “Over here.” The cloud moved above the flowers and let down a gentle rain.

“Mina, is this umbrella yours?” her mother asked. “No, it isn't mine. It belongs to my new friend,” Mina answered. That night, the cloud slept under its umbrella, and Mina fell asleep to the sound of rain.
