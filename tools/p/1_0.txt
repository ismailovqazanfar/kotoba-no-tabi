Title: The Mysterious New Teacher

One spring morning, a new teacher came to the university. He stood in front of the classroom with a blue bag. The students knew neither his name nor which country he came from.

The teacher wrote “Mori” on the board. Then he said, “Nice to meet you. I'm Mori.” His voice was gentle, but when he smiled, a faint sound came from inside his bag.

Ali thought the teacher was Japanese. When Emma asked, “Are you Japanese?” Mr. Mori answered, “No, I'm not Japanese.” The two looked at each other.

This time, Ali tried to guess the teacher's age. When he asked, “Are you thirty?” the teacher slowly shook his head. “I'm two,” he said, and the classroom suddenly went quiet.

Just then, the bag made another sound. When the teacher opened it, there was one big battery inside. He took it out and opened a little door in his chest.

“I am a robot. I came from this university's laboratory,” said Mr. Mori. Researchers at the university had built him two years earlier. Emma gazed at the blue lamp glowing in his chest.

Mr. Mori wrote “teacher” on the board and “student” underneath. “I am a student too. Please tell me your names,” he said. When Emma wrote her name, Mr. Mori smiled again. This time the students laughed along with him.
