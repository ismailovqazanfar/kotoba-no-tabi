Title: The Fox and the Crane's Feast

Once upon a time, a fox and a crane lived in a forest. They were friends, but the fox sometimes played mean tricks. One day, the fox made some delicious soup. Then he said to the crane, “Want to come to my house tomorrow? I'm making something delicious.”

The next day, the crane took a beautiful flower and went to the fox's house. The fox said, “Welcome! Sit over here.” There were two large, flat plates on the table. The plates were filled with warm soup.

The fox lapped up the soup with his long tongue. But the crane had a long beak. He could not drink the soup properly from the flat plate. The fox asked, “What's wrong? It's delicious, but you're not eating?”

The crane felt a little sad, but he did not get angry. He quietly said, “No, I'm hungry. But I'll go home now.” The fox was full and was laughing. On his way home, the crane thought of a good idea.

The next day, the crane invited the fox to his house. He asked, “Would you like to have dinner at my house tomorrow?” The fox replied, “Yeah, I'll come! I'm looking forward to your cooking.” At home, the crane cut up various vegetables and made delicious soup.

When the fox arrived, there were two tall, narrow-necked jars on the table. A wonderful smell came from inside them. The crane put his long beak into a jar and drank the soup happily. But the fox could not put either his nose or his tongue inside.

The fox was troubled and said, “Hey, you're drinking, but I can't.” The crane asked, “Yesterday, I was in the same situation. Do you remember?” The fox remembered what had happened yesterday, and his face turned red. “I'm sorry. I was wrong,” he said quietly.

The crane smiled and took a new plate from the shelf. Then he poured the fox's soup onto the plate. “Now you can drink it. Let's enjoy our meal together,” he said. The two friends ate while laughing. From then on, the fox never played mean tricks again.
