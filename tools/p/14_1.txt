Title: The Giant Robot and the Little Pilot

Fifteen-year-old Sora lived in a town near the sea. Every day, he rode his bicycle to school. In the town’s old factory stood a robot taller than a building. But it had not moved for a long time.

One evening, rain was falling heavily. Outside the factory, Sora heard a quiet voice. “Excuse me, please press the button on the right.” A door in the robot’s leg was slightly open.

Sora stepped inside and pressed the button. A small lift carried him upward. “Please sit in the chair and turn on the light.” In front of him were a map of the town and a blue steering wheel.

Suddenly, a red light came on. “The river is rising. A bus is stopped on the bridge.” Sora looked at the map. His little brother and his school friends were on that bus.

“Shall I drive? But please teach me how.” “Yes. Please hold the steering wheel,” the robot replied. Sora moved it slowly. The giant robot stood up and took one step.

Sora stopped the robot in front of the bridge. “Everyone, please sit down. Please close the bus door.” The robot held the bus in both hands and slowly lifted it. Sora’s hands were trembling a little.

The robot carried the bus to a high hill. “You’re safe now. Please get off one at a time.” The children were waving. His little brother looked up and shouted, “Sora, you’re amazing!”

For the first time, Sora smiled. “Shall I help with anything else?” The robot answered quietly, “Yes. Please look on top of my head.” There, three little cats were waiting in the rain to be rescued.
