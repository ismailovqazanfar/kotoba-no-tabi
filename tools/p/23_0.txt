Title: The Boy Who Lied and the Wolf in the Forest

Long, long ago, a boy who looked after sheep lived in a small village. Every morning, he left the village and walked along a mountain path. When the weather was nice, the sheep ate grass, and the boy rested under a tree. But when he was alone, he felt very lonely.

One day, the boy had an amusing idea. When he looked down at the village from the mountain, he saw many people walking around. He shouted, “Wolf! Help!” again and again. When the villagers heard his voice, they stopped working and ran up the mountain.

The villagers crossed the bridge and turned at the bend in the forest path. But when they reached the mountain, there was no wolf. The boy laughed and said, “Ha ha ha! I was lying!” The villagers became angry and said, “Please don't tell lies.”

The next day, the boy was alone again. When he had nothing to do, he remembered what had happened the day before. Then he shouted, “Wolf!” again. When the villagers arrived, the boy laughed once more. Everyone returned to the village without saying anything.

On the evening of the third day, a strange sound came from the forest. When the boy looked behind him, he saw a big wolf standing there. When the wolf saw the sheep, it slowly came closer. The boy became frightened and quickly climbed a tree.

The boy shouted, “It's a real wolf! Help!” again and again. But even though the villagers heard him, they did not go to the mountain. They thought he was lying again. The boy cried in the tree. The wolf took one sheep away into the forest.

Before nightfall, the boy returned to the village. He told the villagers the truth and said, “I'm sorry.” An old man explained, “If you lie again and again, no one will believe you even when you tell the truth.” The boy quietly bowed his head.

From then on, the boy stopped telling lies. Whenever he looked after the sheep, he worked responsibly. One morning, a loud sound came from the forest, but this time it was not a wolf. A little sheep had simply sneezed very loudly. The boy laughed and said, “The truth can be funny too!”
