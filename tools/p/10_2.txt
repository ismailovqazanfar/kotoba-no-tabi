Title: The Batteries in the Fridge

On Sunday morning, a boy named Sora was at home. It was his grandfather’s birthday. There was a red box on the table. Inside the box was a little toy dog.

There was a switch on the dog’s tummy. Sora turned it on. But the dog did not move. There were no batteries inside the toy. “Where are the batteries?” Sora asked his mother.

His mother was in the kitchen. “They’re in the box on the shelf.” Sora looked inside the white box. There were pencils and old keys. But there were no batteries. “This box?” His mother tilted her head in puzzlement too.

Sora looked under the bed and behind the chair. There was a sock under the bed. There was a cat behind the chair. In front of the cat was something round. But it was not a battery. It was a blue ball.

His little sister Mei was near the door. “Batteries? They’re in the fridge.” Sora was surprised. There were milk and eggs in the fridge. Beside them was a little box with a picture of a battery on it.

Sora opened the box. Inside was brown chocolate. “These are Grandpa’s batteries.” Mei laughed. “Chocolate gives Grandpa energy.” The real batteries were behind that box.

“Because these are Grandpa’s too.” That was why Mei had put the real batteries there as well. Sora put the batteries into the toy. The dog walked across the table. That afternoon, Grandpa came to the house.

In front of Grandpa were the dog and the chocolate. “Do I have batteries too?” Grandpa ate the chocolate. Then he walked around the room with the toy dog. “You work very well!” said Mei.
