Title: The Rainy-Day Lunchbox Contest

In a small town, there lived a girl named Mio. A lunchbox contest was planned for Sunday in the town park. Mio was very excited because it would be her first time entering. Whoever made the tastiest lunchbox would receive a golden spoon.

On Saturday evening, the weather forecast was on television. “There may be heavy rain tomorrow. If it rains, the contest will probably be canceled,” the announcer said. Mio became worried. She asked her mother, “If it's going to rain, should I make my lunchbox at home?”

Her mother said, “If you prepare early, you won't need to rush in the morning. You should cut the vegetables tonight.” Mio cut carrots and cucumbers. But although she made a delicious-looking rolled omelet, she accidentally ate all of it.

The next morning, rain was pouring down. Although she couldn't go to the park, Mio continued making her lunchbox. She thought, “I wish I hadn't eaten all the eggs yesterday.” Just then, she heard an announcement from the town loudspeakers. “The contest will take place in the school gymnasium.”

Mio hurried to cook some rice. But there were no eggs left. Her mother said, “If there are no eggs, why not try making it with just vegetables?” Mio shaped carrots into flowers and made a little forest out of cucumbers.

Many lunchboxes were lined up in the gymnasium. Mio felt embarrassed because hers was rather small. But the more she looked at it, the cuter the little forest seemed. The boy beside her smiled and said, “Eating this would make me feel as if I were having lunch in a forest.”

An elderly judge tasted Mio's lunchbox. “Delicious! And it never rains in this forest,” she said. Everyone laughed. When the results were announced, Mio received a special prize. It wasn't a golden spoon, but a little green umbrella.

When they went home, it was still raining. Mio opened the green umbrella, but it was so small that only her head could fit beneath it. Her mother laughed and said, “That umbrella would be better for your lunchbox!” Mio laughed too. She decided that at the next contest, she would prepare an umbrella for her lunchbox so that even rain wouldn't cause trouble.
