Title: The Little Tempura Shop in Town

In a little town, there was a small tempura shop. The elderly woman who ran it made delicious tempura every day. She could prepare not only vegetable tempura but also fish tempura. However, she could not read books with small print.

One day, a new building was completed across from the shop. It was a large restaurant. Many customers went there, but only three came to the old woman's shop. She felt a little lonely.

That evening, her granddaughter Hana came to the shop. Hana asked, “Grandma, can you do something new?” Her grandmother replied, “I can make tempura, but I cannot make new dishes.” Hana thought for a moment.

The next day, Hana brought a large sheet of paper. It had cute pictures of vegetables. The letters were big, so her grandmother could read them. The paper said, “We can make the tempura you like.”

The first customer was a little boy. He said, “I only want carrot tempura.” The grandmother made tempura using only carrots. The boy happily exclaimed, “I can eat vegetables when they are like this!”

Next came a woman who did not like fish. She ate three pieces of vegetable tempura. Then cheerful voices could be heard outside. Through the shop window, a long line of people was visible.

The grandmother was astonished. “I cannot make that much,” she said. But Hana smiled and said, “I can help, too.” Together, they cut vegetables and kept making more and more tempura.

By evening, only one vegetable remained. The last customer was the chef from the large restaurant. He said, “We cannot make tempura like this at my restaurant.” The grandmother smiled and divided the last piece of tempura in half. They ate it together.
