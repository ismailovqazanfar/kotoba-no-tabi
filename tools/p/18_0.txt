Title: Three-Year Sleeping Taro and the Waterway

Long ago, a young man named Taro lived in a village. Taro slept every day. The villagers called him “Three-Year Sleeping Taro.” For three years, Taro did almost nothing.

The village had a big problem. There was no water in the rice fields. Every day, the villagers carried water from a distant river. But they could not manage to carry enough water.

One morning, Taro went outside for the first time in three years. Taro liked looking at mountains, and he gazed at them for a long time. Then he told his mother, “My hobby is looking at mountains.” His mother said, “Really?” in surprise.

Taro could see the village from the top of the mountain. Beyond the mountain was a large river. Taro thought of a way to bring the river water to the village. He asked the villagers, “Can you move those rocks on the mountain?”

The villagers said, “We cannot move rocks that big.” But Taro did not give up. Before moving the rocks, he dug some soil on the mountain. Then he dug holes beneath the large rocks, too.

The next day, Taro called the villagers. “Please help me. Let's make a water channel together,” he said. Everyone helped move the rocks. Three days later, a small water channel was ready.

Water flowed from the mountain into the rice fields. The villagers were delighted. One person asked, “Taro, what were you doing for three years?” Taro smiled and answered, “I liked looking at the mountains and thinking about a path for the water.”

After that, the village grew plenty of rice. Taro started sleeping until noon again. But the villagers were no longer angry. Every morning, delicious food was waiting outside Taro's house.
