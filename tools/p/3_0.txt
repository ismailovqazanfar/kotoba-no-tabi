Title: Urashima Taro and the Sea Palace

Long ago, a young man named Urashima Taro lived near the sea. One day, some children were tormenting a little turtle on the beach. “That turtle is alive, you know,” Taro said, and rescued it.

The next morning, a large turtle came to the beach. “Please visit the sea palace as my thank-you,” it said. Taro climbed onto its back. Beneath the blue sea stood a palace with a red roof.

At the palace reception, a beautiful princess was waiting for Taro. “This is Ryugu Palace. What is your name?” she asked. Taro told her his name and asked, “Are we under the sea?”

“Yes. This is the first floor of the palace,” the princess replied. “Where is the dining room?” Taro asked. “It is that way. Your room is over there,” she said.

Fish were dancing in the dining room. “Over there is the fish school. That is the turtles’ classroom,” the princess explained. Three happy days passed. But Taro remembered his mother at home.

“Which way is your home?” the princess asked. Taro pointed toward the surface of the sea. She gave him a small box. “This is a gift for you. But please do not open it,” she said.

He returned to the beach with the turtle, but the village looked unfamiliar. “Where is my house? Is this my village?” Taro asked. A villager replied, “Urashima Taro lived three hundred years ago.”

Filled with sadness, Taro opened the box. White smoke rose out, and he became an old man. Just then, the turtle lifted its head from the sea. “This way, Taro,” it called in the same gentle voice as before.
