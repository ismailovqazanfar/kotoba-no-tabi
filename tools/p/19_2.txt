Title: The Diet Starts Tomorrow

A man named Ken lived in a small house in town. Ken loved sweet snacks and big cakes. One day, he checked his weight for the first time in a while and found that he had become heavier. Ken declared, “My diet starts tomorrow!”

The next morning, Ken woke up early and went to the park. He ran, walked, and exercised. But after about ten minutes, he was very tired. When he returned home, he was hungry and ate three large bread rolls.

That day, Ken met his friend Mika. Mika had tried dieting before. “It's good to exercise a little every day,” Mika said. They went for a walk together and drank tea.

Ken changed his lifestyle a little. In the mornings, he cleaned his room and washed his clothes. At lunchtime, he walked to a nearby park, and in the evenings, he ate plenty of vegetables. Gradually, his body felt lighter and his condition improved.

One month later, Ken checked his weight again. He had become a little lighter, but he still had a big belly. Ken felt disappointed. However, he could run longer than before and had become more energetic.

Ken's birthday was coming soon. Mika held a small party for him. There were vegetable dishes and fruit on the table. Everyone said, “Cheers!” and it became a lovely evening.

But at the end of the party, Mika brought out a huge cake. Written on it were the words, “Congratulations on One Month of Dieting!” Ken opened his eyes wide and said nothing for a while. Then he laughed and said, “Today is a special day!”

Ken cut the cake into small pieces and shared it with everyone. The next morning, he went to the park again and enjoyed his run. Mika asked, “How are you feeling?” Ken replied, “Thanks to you, I'm doing great. But my next diet starts tomorrow!” Everyone laughed.
