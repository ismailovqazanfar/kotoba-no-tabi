Title: Grandma's Last Delivery

In a small town, there was a girl named Yuki who worked at a flower shop. Every morning, Yuki delivered flowers by bicycle. One day, an elderly woman came into the shop. She bought one red flower and asked, “Please deliver this to the station tomorrow morning.”

Yuki asked, “Who should I give it to?” The woman answered, “To the person who comes at nine in the morning.” There was no name or phone number. Yuki was a little troubled, but she accepted a letter along with the flower.

The next morning, when Yuki left home, it was raining heavily. Even though it was raining, she rode her bicycle to the station. But when she arrived, it was already ten past nine. The train had already left. Yuki thought, “If only I'd come earlier, I could have met that person.”

Yuki waited at the station with the flower in her hand. Even though she had time to spare, she did not go home. Then a little boy came over. “Whose flower is that?” he asked. When Yuki explained, the boy smiled.

The boy said, “My grandfather is going to a distant town today.” Then he added, “Yesterday, Grandma said she wanted to send him a flower even if she couldn't see him.” Yuki showed the boy the letter. “This letter is for Grandpa,” he said.

Yuki went to the next station with the boy. Even though they did not have much money, they took the train. When they arrived, they saw an old man with white hair. He had missed his train because he had stopped to help a little dog on the road.

When the old man received the flower and letter, tears filled his eyes. The letter said, “Even though your job transfer is taking you far away, please stay healthy every day. Thank you for fifty years.” The old man said, “Actually, today is our fiftieth wedding anniversary.” Yuki was astonished.

That evening, when Yuki returned to the shop, the elderly woman was waiting. She said, “I thought my husband would surely smile if the flower reached him.” Yuki replied, “Both your husband and the dog were doing fine.” The woman laughed and said, “He's been like that since he was young. Whenever he sees a dog, he misses his train!” Yuki burst out laughing too.
