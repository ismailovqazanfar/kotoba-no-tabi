Title: The Ugly Duckling

Once upon a time, a mother duck lived by a pond. One day, six ducklings hatched from their eggs. But one of them was big and gray. The other ducks called that little one Ugly Duckling.

The ugly duckling tried hard every day to become friends with everyone. He practiced many times so that he could swim like the others. But they told him, “Don't come over here.” He was so sad that one morning he left the pond.

In the forest, the ugly duckling saw a white bird. The white bird was flying high in the sky. He wondered, “What is that bird called?” He wanted to know whether he too could fly like that someday.

Before long, winter arrived. Because of the snow, it was difficult to find food. An old man found the cold little bird and brought him home. The old man kindly said, “Stay here until spring.”

The old man fed the duckling every day. He let him rest in a warm room so that he would grow healthy. Thanks to the old man, the little bird gradually grew bigger. Throughout the long winter, he made a habit of looking outside through the window.

When spring came, the old man said, “Go and visit the pond.” The little bird went to the pond just as the old man had told him. There was the white bird he had seen before. “Let's swim together,” said the white bird, coming closer.

“But I'm ugly,” the little bird said quietly. The white bird smiled and asked him to look into the water. When he looked, he saw a beautiful swan. The ugly duckling realized that he himself had become a swan.

“So I was a swan all along!” he cried happily. He no longer had to swim alone. He also learned to fly through the sky with his new friends. From that day on, the swan made sure never to judge anyone only by their appearance.
