Title: The Forgetful Little Wizard

In a small town on a mountain lived a young wizard named Sora. Sora could fly through the sky, but he was terribly forgetful. He often fell asleep with his hat still on or left his bag at home.

One day, the town held a Star Festival. Sora was scheduled to create a hundred lights in the night sky. So he placed his spellbook on his desk to keep from forgetting it. The important magic words were also written on a piece of paper.

But when it was time for the festival, Sora left home without his book. When he reached the square, he opened his bag to check. Inside were only some bread and an old shoe. “Oh no! I forgot my book!” he cried.

Sora hurriedly tried using his magic. But instead of lights in the sky, a huge green frog appeared. The frog jumped up and snatched the mayor's hat. The mayor stood there in astonishment, his hat still missing.

Just then, Sora's friend Mina arrived. “Your spellbook is still at home where you left it,” she said. Sora tried to go back, but his broom would not move. In his hurry, he had forgotten to untie the rope around it.

Without laughing, Mina untied the rope for him. The two climbed onto the broom and flew to Sora's house. The book was on the desk, but the window had been left open. The wind had blown away the paper, so they could no longer find the magic words.

Sora almost began to cry. But Mina said, “Let's try thinking for ourselves without looking at the book.” Sora closed his eyes and remembered everyone's smiling faces. Then he tried a small light spell. A single golden star appeared in the sky.

A hundred little lights sprang from that star, illuminating the whole town. Even the giant frog looked up and croaked happily. The next day, Sora stuck a large note on his bag so he would not forget anything. But the note said, “Don't forget your bag!”
