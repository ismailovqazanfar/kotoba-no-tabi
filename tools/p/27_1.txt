Title: The Little Rocket at Star Camp

One summer, a space camp opened in a small town. A girl named Mio, who loved looking at the sky, planned to attend with her friend Sora. They were thinking of building a small rocket. Every night, Mio looked at the stars and said, “Someday, let's go into space.”

There were plenty of rocket-building materials at the camp. Sora wanted to make a big rocket. But Mio intended to make a small one. “It's better if it's light,” she said, and they decided to use paper and wood.

The next day, everyone began building rockets. Mio and Sora had not attached the engine yet. The teacher said, “We're planning to launch them tomorrow morning.” The two tried to finish everything by evening, but there was not enough time.

Sora became worried and wanted to go home. Mio felt a little sad too. But they decided not to give up. With the teacher's help, they finally completed their little rocket.

In the morning, everyone gathered in a large field. Mio and Sora tried to launch their rocket into the sky. But even when they pressed the button, the rocket did not move. The other children had already launched theirs.

Mio decided to check everything again. Then she found a small disconnected wire. Sora fixed it immediately and said, “Let's try once more.” This time, the rocket flew high and shone against the blue sky.

But suddenly, the rocket flew toward the forest. The two hurried after it. In the forest, a small dog was wagging its tail beside the rocket. The dog thought it was a toy and wanted to take it home.

The dog had no collar. The two decided to take it back to camp. After talking with their teacher, they agreed to look for its owner. Mio laughed and said, “Next year, let's fly even farther. But I'm not planning to launch the dog!”
