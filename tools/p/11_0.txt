Title: Issun-boshi and the Big Bowl

Long ago, an old man and an old woman lived in a village. They had just one child. His name was Issun-boshi, and he was only about three centimeters tall.

Issun-boshi ate ten grains of rice at each meal, three times a day. Every morning around seven, he practiced with his needle sword for about an hour. One day, he set off for the capital in a bowl boat.

The journey to the capital took three whole days. Issun-boshi began working in a large house. About twice a week, he went into town with the princess who lived there.

One day around three, the two went to a temple. There were two enormous ogres in front of the gate. All alone, Issun-boshi stood in front of the princess.

One ogre swallowed Issun-boshi in a single gulp. Inside its belly, it was dark, and there was only one way out. Issun-boshi jabbed the ogre's belly three times with his needle.

The ogre opened its mouth wide. Issun-boshi climbed out through its mouth. The two ogres went back to the mountains, but one small mallet lay on the road.

It was a magic mallet. The princess said, “Grow bigger, grow bigger,” three times. Then she waved the mallet three times. Issun-boshi grew to about the same height as the princess.

That evening, they ate three rice balls each. But Issun-boshi's boat was still a little bowl. “I'll walk home. I'll eat my rice from this boat.” The princess smiled.
