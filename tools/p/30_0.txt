Title: The Stonecutter and the Great Mountain

Long, long ago, a stonecutter lived in a village. Every day, he cut large stones in the mountains. He worked hard on both hot and cold days. But he had very little money.

One day, the man saw a rich person's house. It had a beautiful garden and a large pond. He thought, “If I become rich, I will probably live happily every day.” Just then, the mountain god appeared and made him rich.

The man began living in a large house, but one day he saw the king. The king had many servants. He thought, “If I were king, I would probably be stronger.” The god made him king. But even though he was king, he could not defeat the hot sun.

The man thought, “If I become the sun, perhaps I can do anything.” The god turned him into the sun. He shone brightly from the sky. However, when a thick cloud came, he could no longer see the village below.

The man became a cloud. As a cloud, he could hide the sun. But when a strong wind blew, he was carried far away. He thought, “The wind must be stronger,” and became the wind.

As the wind, the man shook houses and trees. The harder he blew, the stronger he felt. However, one great mountain would not move. He asked the god, “It would be better if I became that mountain.”

The man became a great mountain. Sometimes rivers formed when it rained, but the mountain never moved. He was very satisfied. However, one morning, he heard a small sound beneath his feet.

There was a stonecutter there. With a small tool, he was cutting stones from the great mountain. The man was astonished and thought, “I wish I had stayed a stonecutter.” The god smiled and returned him to his original form. From then on, the man stopped comparing himself with others and valued his work.
