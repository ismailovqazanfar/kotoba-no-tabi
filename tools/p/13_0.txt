Title: The Golden Axe and the Honest Young Man

Once upon a time, a poor young man lived in a village. Every morning, he went to the mountain to cut wood. He had only one old iron axe, but it was very precious to him.

One day, the young man was cutting wood beside a river. The axe fell from his hand into the water. “Oh, I want my axe back. But I don't want to swim in this river. It's deep.”

The young man sat down facing the river. “I want to go somewhere to borrow an axe. But there's nobody here.” Just then, a goddess emerged from the water. “Is something troubling you?”

The goddess held a golden axe. “Do you want this one? Is this the axe you wish for?” The young man answered, “No. My axe is iron. I want to work with my own axe again.”

Next, the goddess showed him a silver axe. The young man shook his head. “Will any axe do?” the goddess asked. “No. I want my old axe.”

At last, the goddess brought out the iron axe. “That's it!” the young man cried. The goddess smiled. “You are honest. I'll give you all three.”

To thank her, the young man went home to cook a meal. Then he came back to the river to meet the goddess. “What would you like to eat?” “Anything is fine. I'm hungry.”

They ate together in the little house. The goddess paid more attention to the rice balls than to the golden axe. “I'd like one more.” The young man laughed. That day, the goddess ate five whole rice balls.
