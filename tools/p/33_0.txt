Title: Hachiko, the Loyal Dog

Long ago, there was a dog named Hachi in Tokyo. Hachi was cared for by a university professor. The professor loved Hachi very much. Hachi loved his master, too.

Every morning, the professor took the train to the university. Hachi walked with him to Shibuya Station. When the professor patted his head, Hachi wagged his tail happily. In the evening, he returned to the station to meet him.

One day, the professor suddenly collapsed at the university. Sadly, he passed away. But Hachi did not know what had happened. At the usual time, he went to the station and waited for his master.

Even when night fell, the professor did not return. The station workers worried about Hachi. Someone from a nearby shop gave him water, but he refused to leave the station. He waited the next day and the day after that, too.

At first, some people did not know Hachi. Once, while he was walking along the street, a child pulled his tail. But Hachi did not get angry. He simply sat quietly in front of the station.

Before long, Hachi's story was written about in a newspaper. He became known throughout Japan. Every day, many people spoke to him and gave him food. They said, “You're waiting for your master again today, aren't you?”

Many years passed. Hachi grew old and began walking more slowly. Even so, he went to the station every day. His loving and loyal heart was praised by many people.

At last, Hachi also passed away. Afterward, people built a statue of him in front of the station. Even today, the statue is loved by many people. Hachi, who once waited for his master, now stands beside people waiting for their friends.
