Title: Monday at Seven

Mina was a student in a small town. On Sunday evening, she put an old clock on her desk. The clock had a cat's face. The next morning, the cat said, "It is seven o'clock now. It is Monday."

She had an important exam on Tuesday. Mina studied at the library from nine in the morning until five in the afternoon. She did not rest that evening either. "I will not sleep tonight. I will study more."

Morning came. But the cat said again, "It is seven o'clock now. It is Monday." On the desk were the same bread and milk as yesterday. Her study notebook was completely blank.

Mina looked at the clock and the calendar. "Yesterday was Monday too. Isn't the exam today?" That day she studied until night again. But at seven the next morning, the same Monday began once more.

On the third Monday, Mina cried. The cat clock spoke in a quiet voice. "What time did you go to bed yesterday?" Mina answered, "I did not sleep. I did not take a break either."

The cat said, "I am a dream clock. I make tomorrow from your dreams." Mina was surprised. "My dreams? Then I will go to bed at ten tonight." The cat's ears pricked up.

Her studying ended at five that afternoon. Mina had dinner with her mother at six. "It is nine thirty now. I will not study any more." She went to bed at ten. Her dream was full of stars.

In the morning, the cat said, "It is seven o'clock now. It is Tuesday." "Tomorrow is finally here." Mina laughed. The cat on the clock said, "Today is my day off," and closed its eyes for the very first time.
