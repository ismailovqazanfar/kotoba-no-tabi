Title: The Old Man Who Made Trees Blossom

Once upon a time, there was a little house near a mountain. A kind old man and woman lived there. They also had a white dog. His name was Shiro.

There was a big tree in the garden. One morning, Shiro was under the tree. “What is here?” The old man dug in that spot. There was a box in the earth.

Inside the box were lots of gold and silver. “This is a present from Shiro.” The old man was delighted. The man next door saw the gold. Then he borrowed Shiro.

There was a tree in the neighbor’s garden too. The man dug beneath it, but there were only old things and rubbish. He became angry and killed Shiro. The old man and woman cried.

Shiro’s grave was in a corner of the garden. The tree above it grew tall. The old man made a rice mortar from its wood. He used the mortar to make mochi. There was gold inside the mochi too.

The neighbor borrowed the mortar too. But there was no gold. He burned the mortar. The old man took the ashes. They were in a little basket.

One day, the old man scattered ashes over a cherry tree. Suddenly, flowers bloomed all over its branches. A lord was standing in front of the tree. “Cherry blossoms? Cherry blossoms in winter?” The lord was amazed.

The neighbor scattered ashes too. But they flew into the lord’s eyes, and the neighbor was scolded. That evening, the old man and woman sat beneath the cherry tree. There were white flowers on Shiro’s grave too.
