Title: The Promise of Tanabata Night

Long ago, Orihime and Hikoboshi lived in the sky. Orihime got up at six every morning. From seven in the morning until five in the afternoon, she made clothes for the stars.

Hikoboshi also worked from morning until night. Looking after the cows was hard, but he never took a day off. The two married, but afterward they spent their time playing together. They did no work.

The stars' clothes were in tatters. The cows were very hungry too. The king of the sky grew angry. He separated the two, placing one east of the Milky Way and the other west of it.

Orihime cried every night. Hikoboshi was unhappy too. The king said, "Once a year, on the night of July seventh, you will both have time off." The two began working every day again.

On the morning of July seventh, Orihime got up at five thirty. "It is five thirty now. Today I will start work early." Hikoboshi also told the cows, "I will not take a break today. Work finishes at six this evening."

At seven that evening, they both came to the river. But it was raining. There was no bridge. "It is seven ten now. Where is Hikoboshi?" Orihime looked at the dark river.

Just then, many magpies arrived. The birds made a long bridge with their wings. The two met in the middle of the bridge. "It is seven thirty now. We are finally together, aren't we?"

They talked until midnight. "I will get up at six tomorrow too." "So will I." When it was time to part, the birds gave them two little feathers. They were a sign of the promise to meet again.
