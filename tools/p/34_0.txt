Title: Kicchomu and the Flying Basket

Long ago, in a village, there lived a clever man named Kicchomu. He always thought of amusing things and made the villagers laugh. But the village had a very mean landlord. Every day, he made the villagers work from early morning.

One day, the landlord called Kicchomu to his house. He showed him a large bamboo basket. “Make this basket fly into the sky. If you cannot, work all day!” he ordered. Kicchomu thought for a moment and asked, “Could you allow me three days to prepare?”

Kicchomu returned to the village and said to a friend, “Could you help me a little?” He had his friend make a long rope and made the children create paper birds. The children worked happily. But one boy could not come because his father was making him study.

On the third morning, Kicchomu carried the basket beneath a tall tree. A long rope ran over the tree and was connected to the basket. He asked his friend, “Please pull the rope when I give the signal.” The villagers gathered, curious to see what would happen.

When the landlord arrived, Kicchomu shouted, “Birds of the sky, make this basket fly!” At once, the basket slowly rose. The paper birds fluttered in the wind. The landlord opened his eyes wide and cried, “It really flew!”

The landlord wanted to go into the sky himself. “Let me ride in it next!” he said. Kicchomu made a troubled face and asked, “In that case, could you allow the villagers to rest for a week?” The landlord was eager to fly, so he replied, “Fine! Hurry up!”

When the landlord climbed into the basket, Kicchomu had his friend pull the rope. The basket rose only a little because the landlord was too heavy. His friend shouted, “I cannot pull anymore!” The frightened landlord almost cried as he shouted, “Get me down quickly!”

Kicchomu lowered the landlord safely. Then he said, “Making everyone laugh is more fun than flying in the sky, isn't it?” The landlord felt embarrassed but kept his promise. The villagers enjoyed a peaceful week of rest, and Kicchomu once again made the whole village laugh.
