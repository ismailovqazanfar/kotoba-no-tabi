Title: Grandma's Mysterious Shopping List

In a certain town lived a boy named Ken. He lived with his grandmother. She was a wonderful cook, always full of energy, and loved by everyone in town. One morning, she said, “Today I'm going to make something special.”

Grandma hummed a song while writing something on a piece of paper. Then she told Ken, “Please go shopping and use this note.” Ken had just come home from school, but he went out cheerfully. The paper said, “Something red, something round, something sweet.”

As Ken walked to the shop, he thought about what to buy. “Maybe the red thing is a tomato. Maybe the round thing is a ball,” he said. At the shop, the owner showed him how to choose good products. But the writing on the note was too small and a little hard to read.

Ken bought a tomato, a small ball, and some chocolate. On his way home, it started raining. He began to run, but the road was slippery, so he decided to walk slowly. As the rain grew heavier, he carefully carried his purchases home.

When Ken arrived home, Grandma was just about to start cooking. He showed her what he had bought. She smiled when she saw the tomato and chocolate. But when she saw the ball, she was so surprised that she burst out laughing. “We can't eat this!” she said.

Grandma took out the real shopping list. It said, “Red apples, round eggs, sweet sugar.” Ken realized his mistake and blushed. “I wrote the letters too small,” Grandma said. Then she showed Ken how to write in large, clear letters.

Laughing together, they went shopping once more. This time, the list had large letters and clearly written names, so they made no mistakes. When they got home, Grandma began making an apple cake. Ken helped by cracking eggs and kept singing until the cake was baked.

When the cake was finished, they ate it together. “Today's shopping was fun,” Ken said. Just then, Grandma brought out the ball. “We've only just finished eating, but let's play a little,” she said. They laughed as they tossed the ball around the garden. As it turned out, Grandma had wanted to play ball for a long time too.
