Title: The Cat Café and the Little Guardian Spirits

In a little town, there was a cat café. White cats and black cats lived inside. Near the window stood an old shelf. The cat on the shelf was always watching the town.

One rainy day, a girl named Mina came to the café. Her house key was missing. There were books and notebooks in her bag, but no key. Mina sat on a chair in front of the window.

The woman at the café gave Mina some warm milk. A white cat was beside Mina’s foot. “A key?” said a little voice. Mina looked around the café. The woman was in the kitchen.

“What? A cat?” Mina looked at the white cat. “Yes. We are this town’s little guardian spirits.” A black cat came out from under the table too. There was a golden bell around its neck.

The white cat went outside with Mina. The black cat came along too. Between the café and the flower shop was a red postbox. “Your key is under that postbox.” There was the little key.

Just then, there was a boy across the street. A dog stood beside him. The boy was crying under a big tree. “That child’s home is near the station.” The black cat’s bell rang.

A man came from the direction of the station. He was the boy’s father. The boy was no longer crying. Mina returned to the café with the cats. “There are lots of guardian spirits in this town.” The cat quietly closed its eyes.

The next day, Mina went to the café again. The white cat was on the shelf. The black cat was under the table. “Are you working today too?” But the only answer was “Meow.” The golden bell gave a soft ring.
