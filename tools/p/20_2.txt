Title: Grandma's New Words

In a town lived a seventy-year-old woman named Mrs. Tanaka. Every day, she looked after her flowers and drank tea. One day, a letter arrived from her grandson Ken, who lived abroad. The letter said, “Next month, I'm coming back to Japan with a friend.”

Ken's friend was from another country and could speak only a little Japanese. Mrs. Tanaka thought, “It might be difficult for him if he doesn't understand Japanese.” But she herself did not know much of any foreign language. So she decided to study English every morning.

Mrs. Tanaka went to a nearby school and asked a young teacher for help. She politely said, “Excuse me. Could you teach me English?” The teacher replied, “Of course. Let's study together.” Mrs. Tanaka wrote various words in her notebook. At home, she even spoke English to her cat.

One morning, Mrs. Tanaka said to her cat, “Good morning!” But the cat only said, “Meow.” Mrs. Tanaka laughed and said, “Are you studying too? But you're still speaking Japanese!” After that, she called her teacher and reported, “I'm practicing every day now. I've learned to speak a little.”

At last, the day of Ken's return arrived. Mrs. Tanaka put on a beautiful kimono and went to the station to meet him. Ken stepped off the train with his friend, carrying a large bag. Mrs. Tanaka politely asked, “Ken, welcome home! Is this your friend?”

Ken said, “Yeah, Grandma. This is John.” Mrs. Tanaka said loudly in English, “Hello! Nice to meet you!” John was surprised but quickly smiled. Then, in very good Japanese, he said, “How do you do? It's a pleasure to meet you.”

Mrs. Tanaka's eyes widened in surprise. “Oh my, your Japanese is very good!” she said. Ken laughed and explained, “Actually, John has been studying Japanese every day. He wanted to talk to you, Grandma.” John added, “I like Japanese food. But kanji is still difficult for me.” The three of them walked home laughing.

When they arrived home, Mrs. Tanaka served everyone tea and sweets. John said, “It's delicious. Thank you very much.” Mrs. Tanaka answered in a more casual way, “Have some more if you like.” Just then, the cat came over and said, “Meow,” to John. John laughed and said, “This cat must be a Japanese teacher!”
