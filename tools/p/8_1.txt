Title: The Soft-Spoken Magical Girl

In a town lived a girl named Rin. She was a quiet child. She did not talk much at school, but she watered the flowers every day. The flowers were her dear friends.

One day, a little cat appeared among the flowers. It was white, but its ears were blue. “Hello. What is the town like?” Rin answered softly. “It's very beautiful.”

The next morning, both the flowers and the sky were gray. The town was not very beautiful anymore. The cat gave Rin a red ribbon. “This is a magic ribbon. From today, you are a magical girl.”

“What kind of girl is a magical girl?” Rin was worried. “Let's see. A kind girl.” “Is magic difficult?” “It isn't difficult. But someone is crying somewhere in town.”

Rin put on the ribbon. Her clothes turned white, but her voice stayed soft. There was a big black cloud in the park. “Are you scared?” the cat asked. “Yes. But I'm going.”

The cloud was crying in front of the flowers. “What do you think of the flowers?” Rin asked. “They're beautiful. But I'm black. I'm not beautiful.” Cold rain fell from the cloud.

Rin sat beside the cloud. “Black cats and black birds are very beautiful too. Your rain is precious water for the flowers.” Her voice was soft, but the cloud stopped crying.

The sky turned blue. The flowers turned red again too. “How do you like being a magical girl?” the cat asked. “It's fun.” Rin's voice was still soft, but beside her, the cloud laughed loudly.
