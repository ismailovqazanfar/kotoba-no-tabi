Title: The Missing Lunch Box and the Little Detective

In a town, there was a girl named Yui. Every morning, her mother made a lunch box for her. There was always a small rolled omelet inside. Yui loved that omelet very much.

One day at school, Yui opened her lunch box. But the omelet was missing. Yui was very surprised. “I wonder if someone ate it,” she thought. So she decided to become a little detective.

First, she asked Takeru, who sat beside her. Takeru said, “It wasn't me.” But there was something yellow around his mouth. When Yui stared at him, Takeru became nervous. It was not egg, though. It was curry.

Next, Yui asked her friend Hana. Hana told her, “A cat came in through the window a little while ago.” Yui thought the cat had eaten her lunch. But there were no crumbs or bits of food on the classroom floor.

Yui asked the teacher, “May I check my lunch box?” The teacher warned her, “You mustn't open your lunch box during class.” Her classmates laughed at her, and Yui felt a little embarrassed. But she still did not give up.

After school, Yui returned home and told her mother everything. Her mother suddenly burst out laughing. “I didn't put any omelet in your lunch box today,” she said. “What? Why?” Yui asked loudly.

Her mother took a small box out of the refrigerator. Inside were many beautifully shaped rolled omelets. They had been made by Yui's father. It was her mother's birthday, and her father had been preparing a special dinner.

That evening, Yui ate the omelets her father had made. Everyone in the family praised him, saying, “Delicious!” Yui forgot all about being warned by her teacher and ate plenty. Then she said, “So Dad was the culprit all along!” and made everyone laugh.
