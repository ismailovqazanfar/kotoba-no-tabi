Title: Princess Kaguya and the Moonlight

Long, long ago, an old man and an old woman lived near a mountain. Every day the old man cut bamboo on the mountain. One morning, in the bamboo grove, there was one stalk shining gold.

When the old man cut the bamboo, there was a tiny girl inside. She was small enough to sit in his hand. He gently carried her home.

The old woman named the girl Kaguya. Kaguya grew up quickly, and the villagers called her Princess Kaguya. She loved the evening hour when she ate supper with the two of them.

But whenever the moon grew round, Princess Kaguya cried in the garden at night. When the old woman sat beside her, the princess said, “I came from the Moon.” On the next full-moon night, the people of the Moon would come for her.

On the night of the full moon, a white cloud came down into the garden. The people of the Moon were standing on it. The old man stood in front of the door, but Princess Kaguya gently took his hand.

“Grandpa, Grandma, thank you,” said Princess Kaguya. Then she handed the old woman a little letter and climbed onto the white cloud. When the two waved, the princess on the cloud waved back.

The letter said: “Please look at the moon. I will look at the same moon too.” After that, on moonlit nights, the two went out into the garden and looked at the sky together. The moonlight gently lit their hands.
