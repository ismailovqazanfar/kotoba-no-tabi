Title: The Disappearing Blue Umbrella

A girl named Hana lived in a small town. Every morning, she bought bread at a bakery near the station. One morning, the sky grew dark. Her mother said, “It's going to rain, so take your umbrella.”

Hana took her blue umbrella and went out. A box for umbrellas had been placed by the bakery entrance. Hana put her umbrella inside. The shop was filled with the lovely smell of sweet bread.

When Hana came outside with her bread, it was raining. She looked in the box, but her blue umbrella was gone. “I wonder if someone took it,” she thought. Holding the bag of bread over her head, she ran home.

By the time Hana reached home, her clothes and shoes were soaked. Her mother closed the window and handed her a towel. “I've lost my umbrella,” Hana said. Instead of getting angry, her mother replied, “Let's look for it again tomorrow.”

The next morning, Hana went straight to the bakery without buying bread. A sign outside said, “Lost items are kept inside.” Hana entered and asked the elderly shopkeeper about her umbrella. Smiling, the woman led her into a small room.

Five blue umbrellas were lined up in the room. Hana opened them one by one, but none was hers. Then she heard a “Meow” from a corner of the room. A cat was sleeping under a piece of blue cloth.

Looking closely, Hana realized that the blue cloth was her umbrella. It had been left open and had become a tiny home for the cat. The shopkeeper explained, “A wet kitten came in yesterday, so I opened the umbrella and left it out for her.” Without waking the kitten, Hana gently stroked her.

Hana decided not to take the umbrella home and to lend it for one more day. The next day, arrangements were made for the kitten to come and live at Hana's house. Her mother prepared a little bed for the kitten. From then on, Hana sometimes opened her umbrella even on sunny days. Whenever she did, the kitten crawled underneath and happily fell asleep.
