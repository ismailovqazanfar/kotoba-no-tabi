Title: The Rolling Rice Ball

Long ago, an old man and an old woman lived in a village. One morning, the old man went to the mountain alone. It was an hour away on foot. The old woman's rice balls were his lunch that day.

At noon, the old man ate his rice balls under a tree. One rice ball rolled away and fell into a little hole. From inside came a cheerful song: "Roll, rice ball, roll! Plop into the hole!"

"Oh, is this a mouse's home?" The old man looked into the hole. Just then, he fell in too. At the bottom, a white mouse said, "Welcome. Please come this way."

The old man walked down a long hallway with the mouse. "Did you come by train? Did you come by bus?" "No, I came to the mountain on foot. Then I came here with my rice ball." The mouse laughed out loud.

The mouse's family was in the back room. "Where are you going today?" the old man asked. "We're not going anywhere. It's our festival today!" The old man sang with the mice.

"When are you going home?" asked the white mouse. "Well, let's see. It's already five. It's about time I went home." The mouse gave him a little box. The old man climbed out of the hole with it.

The old man returned home from the mountain. Inside the box were lots of gold coins. But he talked more about the mice's song than about the money. The old woman smiled too.

The next day, the old man went to the mountain with the old woman. Together, they put a big rice ball into the hole. "There are two of us today!" From below came singing even louder than the day before.
