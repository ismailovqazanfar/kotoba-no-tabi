Title: Grandma and the New Gym

On Sunday, Haru went to a new gym with his grandmother. She had white hair and was short. After breakfast, Haru put a towel in his bag.

They took a bus and got off at the station. Then they changed to a train. “How do we get there from the station?” his grandmother asked. Haru checked the map and pointed to a bright building.

After entering the gym, they received cards at reception. “Please show me how to use this machine,” his grandmother said. The attendant inserted a card and pressed the green button.

“Which room do we exercise in?” his grandmother asked. The attendant showed them a large room. It had big windows and a clean floor. They looked around, then put down their bags.

Haru stepped onto a treadmill. But there were many buttons, and he did not know how to use it. “Which is the stop button?” His grandmother tilted her head in puzzlement too, and they called the attendant.

After asking the attendant, Haru began to walk slowly. “I’ll go and buy some water,” his grandmother said, leaving the room. After five minutes of walking, Haru sat down. His legs already hurt.

His grandmother returned with water. Then she said, “I’ll go and look in the next room.” A young teacher was teaching dance there. As soon as his grandmother heard the music, she began to dance.

His grandmother was flexible and a wonderful dancer. In fact, she had once been a dance teacher. After taking a shower, Haru asked, “Please teach me how to dance like that.” His grandmother laughed. “First, let’s walk for five minutes.”
