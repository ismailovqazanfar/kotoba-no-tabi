Title: Grandma's Secret Move

In a town, there lived a girl named Yuki. Next door lived a grandmother who was always cheerful. Every morning, the grandmother gave Yuki flowers. Yuki sometimes helped her with shopping, too.

One day, Yuki saw many large boxes in front of the grandmother's house. The grandmother was preparing to move. Yuki felt sad and asked, “Are you going far away?” But the grandmother smiled and said, “It is still a secret.”

The next day, Yuki brought her friend Ken. Together, they helped the grandmother carry boxes. Ken repaired a small broken table for her. The grandmother made a delicious cake for both of them.

Yuki asked, “Is there anything else we can help with?” The grandmother said, “Thank you. But I will prepare this myself.” Then she showed them an old photograph. It showed the grandmother when she was young, together with a family from another country.

Long ago, the grandmother had stayed with a host family abroad. At that time, the family had shown her many places. They loved Japanese food. The grandmother told the two children about those memories, but she did not tell them where she was moving.

On Sunday, moving day arrived. Yuki and Ken planned to take the grandmother to the station. But she said, “I am not going to the station.” Then she took the two children to an old bakery in town.

When they opened the bakery door, they saw new tables and flowers inside. The grandmother said, “This is my new home.” Then she introduced them to her friends from abroad. They were the children of the family she had once stayed with.

The grandmother opened a little café there. She smiled and said, “I wanted to create a place where everyone could come together, all by myself.” Yuki and Ken visited every week and helped the customers. The grandmother gave them cake, although she sometimes mixed up the salt and sugar.
