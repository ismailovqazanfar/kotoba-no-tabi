Title: The Teenage Detective and the Missing Key

Fifteen-year-old Ren lived in a small town. At school, he was known as the “teenage detective.” One day, his friend Mina was looking at an empty box outside the music room.

“The key is gone. I must return it to the teacher by three.” Mina looked close to tears. Ren said, “You don't need to worry. It's only two.”

Ren leaned close to the box. “Please don't move this box. First, we must examine the area.” Beside the box lay one small white feather.

The window was open. Without making a sound, Ren looked outside. In the garden was a white bird with something red in its beak.

“The key has a red ribbon,” Mina said. The two walked into the garden without rushing. In a quiet voice, Ren said, “Please don't frighten the bird.”

The bird was in a low tree. “We don't have to climb the tree. Let's wait here.” They waited quietly without taking their eyes off the bird.

At last, the bird dropped the key. Ren picked it up and said, “We must tell the teacher what really happened.” They returned the key by three.

The teacher looked at the red ribbon and laughed. “We can leave the ribbon off. That bird loves red things.” Ren's red cap went straight into his bag.
