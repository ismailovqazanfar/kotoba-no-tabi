Title: The Piper and the Children Who Came Home

Once upon a time, there was a town full of mice. The mice ate bread and ran around inside people's houses. There were far too many mice, and the townspeople were troubled every day.

One day, a man wearing a blue hat came to town. He carried a flute. “I will lead the mice out of town with this flute,” he said. He began walking slowly while playing it.

Then the mice came out of the houses. They kept following the man until they all crossed to the other side of the river. The town became quiet. The townspeople were happy, but they forgot their promise to the man and did not pay him.

The next morning, the man began playing his flute again. This time, the children started following him while singing. The flute sounded beautiful and cheerful, and the children were enchanted. While playing, they reached a flower field far from town.

Back in town, the mothers began searching for their children. “Where are the children?” they asked through tears. The mayor said, “We were wrong not to keep our promise.” The townspeople hurried to the flower field with money and bread.

In the flower field, the children were just making flower crowns. The man was resting under a tree. The mayor handed him the money and asked him to teach them how to play the flute. “Promises are important,” the man said with a smile.

The children had just returned to town and were still a little tired. But they were all well and none were hurt, so their mothers felt relieved. The man taught the children a simple way to play the flute. They began practicing, but making a sound was difficult at first.

That evening, the town held a little concert. As soon as the children finished playing their flutes, they burst into laughter. One child's flute made a strange “Poo!” sound. But it was the funniest sound of all, and the townspeople clapped while laughing. From then on, the town valued keeping promises and practicing music with joy.
