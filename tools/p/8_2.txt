Title: Time to Go Home and the Little Guest

On Sunday, Yuta and his mother visited their new neighbor. It was Hana's house. It was a small house with white walls and a blue door. Yuta brought some sweets in a red box.

Hana was very kind. “How is your new house?” his mother asked. “It's small, but it's very nice.” The room was quiet. Yuta looked at an old clock.

“What kind of clock is this?” “It was my grandfather's clock. It's old, but it still works well.” The clock was shaped like a birdhouse. Yuta drew the interesting clock on a piece of paper.

Hana served tea and cake. “How is the cake?” “It's delicious. It isn't too sweet,” his mother said. It was a lovely afternoon, but it was already five o'clock.

“We should be going,” his mother said. “Yes, I suppose it is time to go home.” Just then, a voice came from inside the clock. “It's still early!” Yuta looked at his mother's face.

“Whose voice was that?” Hana opened the clock's little door. A green bird was inside. “This is Piko.” “What kind of bird is Piko?” “A very noisy one.”

Piko came over to the table. “How is the cake?” he said in a voice just like Yuta's mother's. Everyone laughed loudly. “This clock certainly isn't quiet.” Yuta was no longer worried either.

His mother thanked Hana once more for having them. This time, Piko also said, “I should be going.” Then he perched on Yuta's head. Apparently, it was time for the little guest to go home with them too.
