Title: The Secret of the Delicious-Looking Curry

In a town lived a boy named Kenta. His father was a very good cook. But Kenta had never made curry by himself. One day, his mother said, “Tomorrow is your father's birthday.”

Kenta decided to make curry for his father. In the cookbook, the curry in the picture looked delicious. “I should be able to make this,” Kenta thought. His mother told him, “I've heard this curry is easy enough for children to make.”

The next day, Kenta washed the vegetables and cut them into small pieces. Then he put the meat and vegetables into a pot. After a while, a wonderful smell began to fill the kitchen. “It looks like it'll be ready soon,” Kenta thought happily.

But when Kenta tasted the curry, it was very spicy and a little bitter. He was shocked. “I'm sure I followed the book,” he thought. Apparently, he had mixed up the sugar and the hot spice powder.

Kenta looked as though he might cry. Just then, his grandmother arrived. “They say adding a little milk makes curry less spicy,” she said. Kenta added some milk and stirred slowly.

That evening, Kenta's father came home. On the table was a curry with a rather unusual color. “Wow, that looks delicious!” his father said and took a bite. But suddenly, his eyes widened, and he stopped speaking.

Kenta thought, “I knew it tasted awful.” But his father laughed and said, “This is just like Grandpa's curry!” Apparently, when his father was a child, Grandpa had made exactly the same mistake. That curry, too, had reportedly been pale and very spicy.

His father took another bite and said, “This is the best birthday ever.” Kenta tried some too, and sure enough, it was terribly spicy. But everyone drank water and laughed together. On his birthday the following year, his father said, “I'd love to have that white curry again.”
