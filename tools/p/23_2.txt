Title: Grandma's Mysterious Directions

One Saturday, a university student named Yuki moved to a new town. Her new room was quiet and had a large window. But she did not know the nearby roads very well yet. That morning, her mother called and said, “Remember to visit Grandma near the station.”

Yuki tried to look up the way to the station. But her phone was broken, so she could not see the map. While she was wondering what to do, she noticed a small flower shop on the corner. When she asked the elderly shopkeeper for directions, the woman smiled and helped her.

The elderly woman said, “If you walk straight along this road, you'll reach a large intersection.” She continued, “When the traffic light turns green, cross the road. Then turn right, and you'll see an old bridge. Cross the bridge, and you'll reach the station.” Yuki thanked her and started walking.

Yuki followed the directions exactly. But when she crossed the bridge, she found a large park instead of the station. Inside the park were an old building and a spacious parking lot. “Did I take the wrong way?” Yuki wondered. Just then, a little dog appeared in front of her.

The dog wore a red collar. The name “Momo” was written on it. When Yuki called its name, the dog ran happily. When Yuki stopped, the dog stopped too. Yuki laughed and said, “Are you trying to show me the way?”

When Yuki followed the dog, she found a small house deep inside the park. The elderly woman from the flower shop was standing in front of it. Yuki was surprised and asked, “Why are you here?” The woman laughed and replied, “This is my house.”

Just then, Yuki's mother came out of the house. “Yuki, are you surprised?” she asked. In fact, the elderly flower shop owner was Yuki's own grandmother. Yuki had not seen her for many years, since she was a child, so she did not remember her face. Her mother and grandmother had prepared a little party for her.

While everyone was eating cake, Yuki asked her grandmother, “But why didn't you tell me the way to the station?” Grandma laughed and said, “Coming here is more fun than going to the station, isn't it?” Momo barked loudly, “Woof!” From that day on, whenever Yuki got lost, she asked people for directions instead of relying only on maps.
