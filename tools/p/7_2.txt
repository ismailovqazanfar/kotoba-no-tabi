Title: A Present for the Teacher

On Saturday, Sora received a sheet of paper from his mother in the kitchen. It said, “Sunday is the teacher’s birthday.” His mother took cooking lessons every week from a teacher in the neighborhood.

Sora had not met this teacher yet. “I will give the teacher a present,” he said. “That sounds nice. I have already bought flowers,” his mother replied.

Sora borrowed scissors from his older sister. She lent him her colored pencils too. Sora cut the paper with the scissors. Then he drew a red flower with the colored pencils.

“What is ‘arigatou’ in English?” Sora asked his sister. She taught him “thank you.” Sora learned the English words from her, but wrote “thank you” in Japanese on the card.

Next, Sora bought a large blue shirt at a shop. “What is ‘sensei’ in English?” he asked the shop assistant. The assistant taught him “teacher.” Sora wrote the word on the gift bag.

On Sunday, his mother asked, “Have you written the card yet?” “Yes, it’s done. I have a present too,” Sora replied. They took the flowers and the large bag to the house next door.

A little girl opened the door. His mother gave her the flowers. “This is my cooking teacher. She is eight years old,” she said. Sora looked at his large bag.

The girl received the shirt from Sora. “I’ll wear this when I cook. Thank you,” she said with a smile. That day, Sora took a cooking lesson from her too. The teacher’s blue shirt reached all the way to her knees.
