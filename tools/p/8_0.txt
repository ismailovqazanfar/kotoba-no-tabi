Title: The Snow Woman and the Red Gloves

Long ago, Minokichi and an old man lived in a cold mountain village. They worked in the mountains every day. One day, a great deal of white snow fell. The mountain path was already completely white.

They went to an old hut. It was small, but it was not very cold inside. The old man soon went to sleep. Minokichi gazed alone at the black sky.

That night, the door opened. A woman in a white kimono came inside. She was very beautiful, but her hands were blue. “Good evening. I am the Snow Woman.” Suddenly, the hut grew cold.

“What kind of person is a snow woman?” Minokichi asked in a small voice. “Let me see. A quiet person. I am not a bad person.” The Snow Woman looked at the old man. “He is asleep already, isn't he?”

The Snow Woman's hands were very cold. Minokichi took a pair of red gloves from his bag. “How about these gloves? They aren't new, but they are good gloves.” They belonged to his mother.

The Snow Woman put on the gloves. “How are they?” Minokichi asked. “They are very nice. My hands aren't cold anymore.” The Snow Woman smiled for the first time. The red gloves were a little large.

“You are a kind person. Here is a present for both of you.” The Snow Woman brought out two white blankets. Then she returned to the snow. Neither man felt very cold that night.

In the morning, the old man looked at his blanket. “What sort of blanket is this?” “A blanket from a kind person.” Outside, the world was white, but far away on the mountain, two tiny red gloves were moving.
