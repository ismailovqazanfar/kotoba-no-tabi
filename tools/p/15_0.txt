Title: The Warbler’s Village and the Three Eggs

Long ago, an old man lived near a mountain. He made baskets from bamboo. One spring day, he lost his way deep in the mountain.

Beyond the trees stood a fine house. A young woman was standing at the gate. The old man asked, “Excuse me. May I rest here?”

The woman welcomed the old man inside. “I live here. I know the mountain paths well.” Then she placed warm tea and sweets on the table.

Soon the woman said, “I’m going out for a little while. But you must not enter the back room.” The old man asked, “May I look at the garden?” “Yes, the garden is fine.”

The flowers in the garden were lovely. But a faint sound came from the back room. The old man opened the door a little. Inside were three girls wearing beautiful clothes.

When the girls saw the old man, they turned into birds and flew away. There were three eggs on a shelf in the room. “What kind of eggs are these?” The old man picked one up.

Just then, the egg slipped from his hand. The other two fell from the shelf as well, and all three broke. The woman returned. “My family lives here. You must not touch the eggs!”

The woman gave a sorrowful cry and turned into a Japanese bush warbler. The house and garden vanished. Every spring, the old man remembered her voice. He faced the mountain and quietly apologized.
