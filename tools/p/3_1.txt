Title: The Star School Under the Stairs

One evening, Yui was searching for her red notebook at school. It was neither in her classroom nor in the dining hall. Under the stairs on the first floor was a little blue door. A star-shaped piece of paper slipped out from beneath it.

When Yui opened the door, she saw the night sky. A school stood on a white cloud. A little boy asked, “This is the star school. What is your name?” His shoes shone gold.

The boy’s name was Lulu. “Where is the classroom?” Yui asked. “This is reception. The classroom is over there,” Lulu replied. But the classroom was completely dark, and the star children were crying.

“Our school’s star is missing,” the teacher said. That star provided the school with its light. “Which way is your room?” Yui asked. “That way. But it is not there either,” the teacher replied.

Yui and Lulu walked along the dark corridor. “The dining hall is over there. The office is on the second floor,” Lulu said. Just then, Yui heard a faint sound. It came from inside Lulu’s shoe.

“What is inside that shoe?” Yui asked. Lulu took it off. A little star was sleeping inside. “It is warm here,” the star said, and everyone laughed.

The star returned to the classroom ceiling. The school became bright again. The teacher handed Yui a red notebook. “Is this your notebook? It was here at reception,” he said.

Yui thanked everyone and went back through the blue door. The next morning, there was only an ordinary wall under the stairs. But inside her notebook was a star-shaped piece of paper. In tiny letters, it said, “Where is your classroom?”
