Title: The Hare and the Tortoise

Once upon a time, a hare and a tortoise lived in a forest. The hare was fast, but the tortoise was very slow. The forest was warm and quiet in spring.

One day, the hare asked everyone, “Who is the fastest animal in the forest? I am, of course!” The tortoise smiled a little.

“Then shall we race to the top of that mountain?” said the tortoise. The mountain was not close. “All right. I wasn’t free yesterday, but I am today,” answered the hare.

The fox raised a white flag. The hare was much faster than the tortoise and soon went far ahead. The tortoise walked slowly past large and small stones.

Two trees stood beside the path. “Which is cooler, the big tree or the small tree?” the hare asked a bird. “It’s cooler under the big tree,” the bird answered.

The hare rested under the big one. It was not hot there, so he soon fell asleep. The tortoise did not stop to rest. The path was hotter at noon than in the morning, but he went on one step at a time.

In the late afternoon, the hare woke up. The tortoise was already near the mountaintop. The hare hurried too, but he was not first. The little tortoise was first.

At the top of the mountain, they ate rice balls. The tortoise gave the big one to the hare. “Which do you like better, racing or taking naps?” the tortoise asked. With a red face, the hare answered, “Right now, I like rice balls best.”
