Title: The Starry Stage and the Three Friends' Promise

In a small town near the sea lived three girls named Mio, Hana, and Yuki. All three loved singing and dancing, and dreamed of becoming idols someday. One day, the date of their very first concert was decided.

Every day after school, the three girls practiced. Mio danced while singing, and Hana memorized the moves while listening to music. Yuki practiced her hand movements while looking in the mirror. The new song was easy to remember, but the dance was a little difficult.

Three days before the concert, Mio practiced too much. As she kept singing, her voice grew weak. “It's hard to get my voice out,” she said. Hana's foot had also just started hurting. All three girls became very worried.

Just then, their teacher entered the practice room. The girls were about to start a new dance. “Practice is important, but resting is important too,” the teacher said. Then the teacher showed them a less tiring way to dance and how to take care of their voices.

The next day, the girls began practicing at a gentle pace. They took breaks while drinking water and sang short songs. The room was bright and the teacher was kind, so everyone gradually felt better. By evening, they had finished learning their final dance.

At last, concert day arrived. The girls had just put on their beautiful costumes and were still a little nervous. “What if there are too many people?” Yuki whispered. But when the music began, all three burst into song with smiles on their faces.

But during the second song, the music suddenly stopped. The girls were startled, but Mio began clapping her hands. Hana and Yuki kept singing while clapping too. The audience joined in, and the hall filled with loud singing.

When the girls finished their final song, thunderous applause rang out. Their teacher was smiling through tears. “That was a wonderful concert,” the teacher said. The girls held hands onstage. Many stars shone that night, but to the three girls, the audience's smiles were the brightest stars of all.
