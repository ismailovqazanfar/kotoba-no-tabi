Title: The Secret Summer Vacation Plan

A girl named Yuki lived in a small town. She was thinking of going to the sea during summer vacation. Her mother and father planned to go with her. Yuki had bought a new swimsuit, but had not packed it yet.

But three days before the trip, her father had an important job to do. Their plan to go to the sea was canceled. Yuki felt like crying, but she intended not to cry. “Then let's have fun at home,” she thought.

The next morning, Yuki decided to make a summer vacation plan. First, she tried to set up a small pool in the garden. But the pool had a hole. Even when she filled it with water, the water quickly disappeared.

Yuki decided not to use the pool. Instead, she thought of making a big sea out of blue paper. Her little brother Taro wanted to help too. They spread the paper across the floor and drew fish and boats.

In the afternoon, they planned to make rice balls with their mother. Taro wanted to eat a big rice ball. But there was only a little rice. So they decided to make many small rice balls.

That evening, their father came home early. He was very surprised to see the blue sea inside the room. Yuki said, “Tomorrow, let's have a seaside festival here.” Their father also decided not to work on his day off.

The next day, the whole family held their seaside festival. Their mother played music, and their father wore a fish-shaped hat. Taro tried to get into a paper boat, but it was much too small. Everyone laughed and ate rice balls.

That night, their father said, “Next year, let's definitely go to the real sea.” Yuki smiled happily. But they had not decided on next year's plans yet. Taro alone intended to play in their homemade sea again next year. After all, he had already made a hundred paper fish!
