Title: Where Is Mum?

On Sunday, Haru went to a department store with his mother. It was her birthday. Haru had five hundred yen in his pocket. His mother was looking at white shoes in the shoe department on the second floor.

In the next department, Haru found a handkerchief with flowers on it. “How much is this?” he asked the shop assistant. “Three hundred yen,” she replied. Haru took out his money and bought it.

But his mother was no longer beside the white shoes. “Where is Mum?” Haru said quietly. “The information desk is over there,” the shop assistant told him. Holding the handkerchief carefully, Haru went to the desk.

A kind young woman was at the information desk. “What is your name? Where is your mother?” she asked. “I’m Haru. Mum is here. Oh, not here. Over there,” Haru said, pointing to the shoe department.

“Please sit in this chair,” the woman said. “This is the second-floor information desk. The office is that way,” she explained. Haru sat down to wait for his mother. But he was a little worried.

Just then, the telephone rang. It was the first-floor information desk. At the other end, a woman was asking, “Where is Haru?” The young woman smiled and replied, “Haru is here with us.”

Soon, his mother stepped out of the lift. “So here you are,” she said, hugging Haru. “Mum, is your information desk on the first floor? Mine is on the second floor,” Haru said. Both his mother and the young woman laughed.

Haru gave her the handkerchief and said, “Happy birthday.” A little tear shone in his mother’s eye. The new handkerchief came in useful straight away. Haru took her hand, and this time they walked together.
