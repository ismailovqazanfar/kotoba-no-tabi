Title: The Young Samurai and the Moon Sword

Long ago, there was a small sword-training school on a mountain. A young samurai apprentice named Haru lived there. Haru's master was very strict. Every morning, he made Haru run and swing a wooden sword one hundred times.

One night, Haru found a sword glowing blue in the forest. A small moon was drawn on its blade. When Haru touched it, he heard a voice saying, “Protect the weak!” Surprised, Haru hurried to show it to his master.

The master looked at the sword and said quietly, “If you want to use that sword, first strengthen your heart.” Then he made Haru clean the courtyard. Haru asked, “Could you allow me to practice with the sword for a little while?” But the master shook his head.

The next day, Haru was made to carry water and clean a long staircase. Exhausted, he thought, “I wish my master would teach me more kindly.” Just then, a small fox entered the courtyard. Its leg was injured, and it cried sadly.

Haru asked his master, “Could you allow me to go and help the fox?” The master replied, “Go. But be careful.” Haru carried the fox to a small hut on the mountain. There, he asked an old woman who made medicines, “Could you please heal this little one?”

That night, black clouds gathered over the mountain, and a strong wind began to blow. A frightening sound came from the forest, and the village children started crying. The master told Haru, “Go to the village and protect the children.” Haru took the Moon Sword and ran down the dark path.

A huge black shadow stood in front of the village. Haru shouted, “Get out of here!” But the shadow did not move. Just then, the fox he had saved appeared and made the Moon Sword shine. In the bright light, the black shadow turned into a tiny cloud and vanished.

The next morning, the master called Haru to the training hall. “Well done. You are a true samurai,” he praised him. Haru smiled and asked, “Then could you allow me to rest today?” For the first time, the master laughed heartily and said, “All right, play with the fox today!” The happy fox jumped onto Haru's shoulder.
