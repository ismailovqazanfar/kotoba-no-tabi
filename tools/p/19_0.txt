Title: The White Rabbit of Inaba and the Kind God

Long, long ago, a white rabbit lived near the sea in Inaba. The rabbit lived on a small island, but he wanted to visit the big mountain across the sea. However, he had never crossed the sea before.

One day, the rabbit saw some large crocodiles in the sea. He asked them to line up in a row across the water. Then he jumped from one crocodile's back to another and reached the opposite shore. The rabbit became very happy.

But when the rabbit jumped from the last crocodile's back, he fell onto a rock. His legs and back began to hurt, and he could not walk. He washed himself in seawater and rested on the sand. But the pain only became worse.

Just then, a kind god named Okuninushi arrived. The god listened to the rabbit's story and spoke gently. “Please don't wash yourself with seawater. Wash with clean river water and rest on the grass.”

The rabbit remembered the god's words. He washed in a small river and slept on soft grass. The next day, his sore legs gradually got better. His white fur became clean again, too.

The rabbit recovered and thanked the god. “Thanks to you, I'm well again!” The god smiled warmly. Then the two went to the mountain, looked at flowers, and listened to birds singing.

From the top of the mountain, they could see the little island across the sea. The rabbit wanted to go home, but he was a little afraid to cross the water again. The god found a small boat and took the rabbit back to his island. The rabbit felt relieved.

After that, the rabbit sometimes traveled to the mountain by boat. He climbed the mountain and played with his friends. Whenever he saw an animal in trouble, he kindly helped. The story of the white rabbit became famous along the coast.
