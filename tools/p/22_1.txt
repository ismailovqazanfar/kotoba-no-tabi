Title: The Yokai and the Mysterious Apartment

In a certain town, there lived a young boy named Sora. Sora was looking for a new apartment. He wanted a room close to school with low rent. One day, he found an old apartment building. It had a room with a large window.

When he entered the room, he saw a little boy wearing a white hat. The boy had on a blue sweater and red shoes. “I'm Mochi. I'm a yokai who lives here,” he said. Sora was surprised, but Mochi had a very cute face.

“Let's live together!” Mochi said. Sora thought for a moment. “All right. But please be quiet at night,” he replied. Mochi nodded eagerly. That night, the two promised to live together. Sora was looking forward to life with a yokai.

The next day, it was time for Sora to go to school. But his glasses were missing. “Mochi, have you seen my glasses?” he asked. “Hmm, they were on the desk yesterday,” Mochi answered. Amazingly, the glasses they were looking for were inside the refrigerator.

That evening, Sora had to make a packed lunch. When he went to the kitchen, his lunch box was missing. “Shall we look for it together?” Mochi asked. Amazingly, the box they found was under Sora's bed. “My magic acts strangely sometimes,” Mochi said with a laugh.

One day, Sora made plans for a friend to visit. “My friend is coming today, so please don't use magic,” Sora asked Mochi. “Okay, I understand,” Mochi replied. But when the friend arrived, the cake on the table began flying through the air. “What an amazing robot!” the friend exclaimed happily.

Sora didn't know what to do, but Mochi was having fun. Sweet candies began falling from a little cloud Mochi had made. “This apartment really is fun!” the friend said, laughing. Mochi puffed out his chest proudly. “I've got a sense of humor!” he declared.

From then on, friends visited Sora's room every day. Mochi showed them lots of magic tricks that made everyone laugh. One morning, Sora woke up and found a little rainbow in the room. Beneath the rainbow was a piece of paper that said, “Our Home.” When Sora saw it, he felt very happy.
