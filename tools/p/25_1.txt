Title: The Time Capsule Ten Years Later

At a small elementary school in the countryside, there were three friends named Haru, Sora, and Mio. They played together every day. One day, their teacher said, “Let's write letters to ourselves ten years from now.” The three put their letters into a large box.

Haru wrote, “When I grow up, I'll build a robot that can fly.” Sora wrote, “If I get the chance, I'll study abroad.” Mio wrote, “Even if I become famous, I won't forget my friends.” Then the three buried the box in the schoolyard.

Ten years passed. Haru was studying robots in the city, but he still had not made a flying robot. Sora had studied abroad and learned many things. Mio had become an elementary school teacher instead of a singer.

One day, Mio called both friends. “Hello, do you remember the box from ten years ago? Please come to the school on Sunday,” she said. Even though Haru was busy with work, he decided to go. Sora lived far away too, but he returned by train.

On Sunday, when the three arrived at school, there was a big tree in the yard. The box was beneath that tree. They removed the soil and finally found the box. When they opened it, they found their old letters and a photograph.

When Haru read his letter, he felt a little sad. “I still haven't achieved my dream,” he said. Mio smiled and said, “Even if your dream hasn't come true yet, that's all right.” Sora added, “If I help you, I'm sure you can do it.” The three decided to try again.

Just then, they noticed a small letter behind the photograph. It was a letter from their teacher. It said, “If all of you can meet again in good health after ten years, that will be the greatest treasure.” The three thought about what those words meant. Then they all laughed together.

Before leaving, the three prepared a new box. This time, they wrote short letters to themselves ten years in the future. Haru wrote, “Even if my robot doesn't fly, I'll look at the sky with my friends.” Just then, a paper robot Mio had made flew into the air on the wind. The three were surprised and laughed again.
