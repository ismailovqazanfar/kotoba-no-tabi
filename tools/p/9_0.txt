Title: The Old Man Who Lost His Lump

Once upon a time, an old man lived near a mountain. He had a large lump on his right cheek. He loved singing and dancing, and he sang many songs in his garden every day.

One day, the old man went to the mountain. Heavy rain fell that evening, so he rested in the hollow of a large tree. At night, lively music came from outside.

Oni were singing in front of the tree. The old man could not understand any of the words, but the music was cheerful. “What kind of dance is that?” He poked his head out of the hollow.

“I like dancing too.” The old man danced merrily before the oni. “You are very good! Why are you so good at it?” they asked.

“I dance a lot every day, so I am fairly good.” The oni chief was delighted. “Promise to come tomorrow too. We will keep this lump here.” With a pop, he took off the lump.

The next morning, the old man went home. His elderly neighbor also had a lump, on his left cheek. The neighbor listened to most of the story, but he did not like dancing very much.

That night, the neighbor went to the mountain too. He was a poor dancer, so the oni were disappointed. “That is not yesterday’s dance. What a pity.” The chief put the first old man’s lump on the neighbor’s right cheek.

The neighbor went home with two lumps. “I hate singing and dancing.” Yet the next day, the two old men practiced dancing together. This time, the neighbor listened carefully.
