Title: The Father Who Wanted to Go Home Early

In a town lived an office worker named Takeshi. He had a seven-year-old daughter named Mio. Every day, Mio wanted to play with her father. But Takeshi's section manager loved working and made everyone stay late.

One Friday, Mio's school was holding a small performance. That morning, Mio said, “You absolutely must come today. I want you to hear my song.” Takeshi promised, “All right. I'll definitely be there.” Then he hurried to the office.

During lunch, Takeshi went to the manager's office. He asked, “My daughter has a school performance. Could you allow me to leave at three today?” However, the manager said, “There is still work to do. Finish these documents first.” Takeshi was troubled, but he quickly started working.

The senior coworker at the next desk noticed Takeshi's worried face. Takeshi asked, “Could you check these numbers for me?” His coworker said, “Of course. You mustn't keep a child waiting.” Working together, they finished all the documents before three.

Takeshi asked the manager once more, “Could you please allow me to leave?” The manager looked at the documents in surprise. “You've finished already? All right, go!” he said. Takeshi thanked him repeatedly and rushed out of the office.

When Takeshi reached the school, the performance had already begun. He quietly asked a teacher, “Could you let me enter from the back?” The teacher smiled and silently opened the door. Mio spotted her father from the stage and sang happily.

But when Mio's song ended, the teacher announced to the audience, “Next, we're going to have the fathers sing too!” Takeshi was shocked and asked the person beside him, “Really?” The fathers whom the teacher had made come to the front were all blushing.

Takeshi was also made to stand on stage and sing in front of the children. He was not a very good singer, but Mio laughed with delight. Suddenly, loud applause came from the back of the hall. To everyone's surprise, Takeshi's manager was standing there, clapping happily!

As it turned out, the manager's grandchild attended the same school. The manager said, “Your singing was funny. From tomorrow, I'll let everyone go home early.” Takeshi felt relieved and held Mio's hand. On the way home, Mio said, “Sing again tomorrow!” But Takeshi laughed and replied, “Anything but that!”
