Title: The Girl Who Made Rain and the Blue Sky

In a small town near the mountains lived a girl named Yuna. She had a mysterious power. Whenever she thought, “It will rain,” rain really fell. But Yuna told nobody about her power. She believed everyone would think it was a lie.

One summer, no rain fell in the town for an entire month. The river grew shallow, and the flowers began to wilt. On the television news, they said, “This year will probably be very hot.” Yuna thought, “I have to make it rain...”

The next day, a big festival was being held in the town square. Yuna thought, “If I make it rain now, everyone will have trouble, won't they?” But the flowers needed water. Yuna looked up at the sky alone in the park. Then she whispered, “Rain, please come.”

Dark clouds gathered in the sky. Rain began falling little by little. But when Yuna sneezed, the rain suddenly became heavy. People at the festival shouted, “What incredible rain!” and ran for shelter. Yuna thought, “This has become a disaster!”

Just then, a little boy was crying in the rain. The wind had blown away his red umbrella. “I have to help him...” Yuna said, running toward him. They went beneath a large tree. The boy said, “Thank you, big sister.”

The boy asked, “When do you think this rain will stop?” Yuna answered, “I think it will stop soon.” Then she wished with all her heart, “Let the sky turn blue!” The rain stopped, and the clouds slowly moved away. A great rainbow appeared in the sky.

The townspeople saw the rainbow and cheered. An announcement at the festival said, “Thanks to the rain, the town's flowers are healthy again.” The boy looked at Yuna and asked, “You're a genius, aren't you?” Yuna smiled and said, “No, I'm just an ordinary girl.”

The next morning, a little flower lay in front of Yuna's house. Beside it was a note saying, “Thank you for the rain.” Yuna wondered, “Who could have left this?” Looking up, she saw a small cloud shaped like a heart. Yuna laughed and said, “I think the clouds have become my friends too.”
