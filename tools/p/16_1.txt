Title: The Magic Camera and the White Cat

A girl named Mina lived in a town. She had short hair and big eyes. One day, after cleaning her room, she opened an old box. Inside was a silver camera.

The camera had a red button and a blue button. “How do you use it?” Mina whispered. Then the camera answered, “It’s easy to use.” Startled, Mina put it on the bed.

Mina put in a sheet of paper and picked up the camera. “Which button takes pictures?” “Press the red button,” said the camera. Mina photographed a drawing of a cat on her desk.

Then a white cat came out of the camera. It had little ears and a long tail. It ran across the room and jumped onto the bed. But there was nothing on the paper.

“Please tell me how to put the cat back,” said Mina. “Photograph it, then press the blue button.” But the cat climbed off the bed and went out into the garden through the window. Mina hurried outside too.

There were three white cats in the garden. “Which cat is it?” asked Mina. “That cat has a blue nose.” Mina spotted the blue nose and quietly sat down nearby.

“I’ll go and get some milk,” said Mina. She went to the kitchen and returned with milk and a little dish. The cat drank the milk and climbed onto her lap. Its little body was warm.

Mina lowered the camera and stroked the cat’s head. That evening, she said, “I’ll go and draw a cat bed.” “This time, put in a big sheet of paper,” the camera replied.
