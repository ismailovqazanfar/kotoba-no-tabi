Title: The Little Witch's Delivery Service

In a seaside town lived a young witch named Lili. She was thirteen and lived with her black cat. One morning, she started a service delivering parcels by broomstick.

During the first week, only about one customer came each day. There was just one parcel a day, too. Lili practiced flying twice a day, for thirty minutes each time.

On Sunday around nine, the baker came. “Please take ten sandwiches to the house on the mountain.” Lili received one large box. There were five apples inside as well.

The house was about an hour away on foot. By broomstick, it was only ten minutes. Lili picked up the box and her broom, but the broom rose only a little.

The box was heavy, and the old broom was small. Lili prepared three boxes. She put five sandwiches into each of two boxes and the five apples into the third.

Lili carried one box at a time. That morning, she flew to the mountain house three whole times. Around eleven, the last box arrived, but there was only one old woman at the door.

“Do you eat ten all by yourself?” The old woman laughed. “No. Nine guests are coming. We have lunch here once a month.” Just then, nine broomsticks landed in the garden.

Every guest was a witch. As a thank-you, Lili received a new broomstick. The next day, ten customers came. Even the black cat took no rest that day.
