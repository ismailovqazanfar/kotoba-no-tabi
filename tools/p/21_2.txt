Title: Grandpa's Secret News

In a certain town lived a boy named Ken. Every morning, Ken watched the television news. He would say, “I think it's important to know what's happening in the world.” But his grandfather, who lived with him, hardly watched television.

Grandpa went for a walk in the park every morning. Whenever he returned, he said, “There was good news today too!” Ken would ask, “But they didn't say anything on television. You're joking, aren't you?” Grandpa laughed and said, “It's true.”

One day, the town's school held a news presentation. The teacher said, “Everyone, please bring an interesting news story.” Ken researched news about cars and traffic. But all his friends brought the same news stories.

At home, Ken said, “I need some new news...” Grandpa asked, “News from the park would be fine too, wouldn't it?” Ken asked, “What happened in the park?” Grandpa said, “Come with me tomorrow morning.”

The next morning, they went to the park together. A little dog was running happily. Grandpa said, “Until yesterday, this dog couldn't walk. But today it's running.” Ken exclaimed, “Amazing! That really is good news!”

Farther inside the park, a little girl was riding a bicycle. Grandpa said, “She practiced for a whole week. Today, for the first time, she rode all by herself.” Ken said, “I think this is more interesting than television news.” Then he wrote the story in his little notebook.

At the school presentation, Ken shared the news from the park. “I think small moments of happiness are important news too,” he said. The teacher replied, “That's a wonderful idea.” His friends said, “Tomorrow, let's look for news around our town too!”

That evening, Ken asked his grandfather, “How do you find good news every day?” Grandpa said, “If you don't look carefully every day, you won't find it.” Then he laughed and added, “But today's biggest news is that Ken woke up early!” Ken protested, “That's not news!” Yet they both kept laughing for a long time.
