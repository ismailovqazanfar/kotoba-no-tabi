Title: Grandma's Japanese Notebook

In a certain town, there lived a boy named Haru. Haru's grandmother had come to Japan from another country. She studied every day so that she could speak Japanese well. Haru had begun teaching his grandmother Japanese.

Grandma said, “I learn three words every day so that I won't forget them.” Haru drew pictures in her notebook and explained the meanings of the words. Grandma wrote the characters many times, exactly as Haru had written them. Thanks to Haru, she gradually learned to read kanji too.

One day, Grandma said, “Tomorrow, I want to go shopping by myself.” Haru told her to ask a shop worker whenever she did not understand a word. Then he made a small shopping note for her. On it, he wrote, “Eggs, milk, bread.”

The next day, Grandma took the note and went to the supermarket. But because she had forgotten her glasses, she could not read the small writing. She did not know which product was milk, so she asked a shop assistant. The assistant smiled and showed her where the milk was.

When Grandma came home, she was carrying a large bag. Haru was excited to see what was inside. But there were no eggs or milk in the bag. Instead, there were three packets of cat food and one large fish.

Haru asked, “Why did you buy cat food?” Grandma replied, “There was a hungry cat in front of the store.” She added, “I felt sorry for it, so I bought it some food.” Haru began wondering whether the cat had eaten enough.

Just then, they heard a “Meow” from the entrance. When Haru opened the door, the same cat came inside. Grandma laughed and said, “It followed me all the way home.” Haru sat quietly so that the cat would not be frightened.

The next morning, Haru asked Grandma to practice shopping once more. Grandma replied, “This time, I'll make sure not to forget my glasses.” But the cat picked up the shopping note in its mouth and ran into the garden. They both laughed and wrote a new sentence in the notebook. It said, “Because of the cat, we're going shopping again.”
