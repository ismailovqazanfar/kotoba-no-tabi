Title: A Disappointing Sunday

Yui liked cooking and was especially good at making curry. She had plenty of time on Sunday, so she called her friend Ken. “Would you like to have curry at my house today?”

“What a pity. I have something to do today,” Ken answered quietly. “What do you have to do?” Ken said, “Shopping,” but did not say much after that.

Next, Yui called her friend Aki. Aki liked cooking too, but she said, “I have plans with someone today, so I won’t come.” Yui ate lunch alone.

That afternoon, Yui went to the shopping street. Ken bought a lot of red flowers at a shop. Aki was in the shop next door. She was holding a large white box.

Neither of them noticed Yui. “Why are they together? Don’t they like me?” Yui could not understand it at all. She felt sad, so she went straight home.

That evening, there was a sound at the front door. Yui opened it. Ken and Aki stood there with the big box and flowers. “Happy birthday!” Yui’s eyes widened.

Inside the box was a slightly oddly shaped cake. “We went to a cake-making class. We’re both bad at it, so it took a long time.” Yui looked closely. The chocolate lettering spelled “Yui.”

“Thank you. But my birthday is next month.” The two friends had mixed up the calendar. Still, the cake was delicious. “Will you have time next month too?” This time, all three laughed.
