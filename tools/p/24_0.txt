Title: The Straw Millionaire

Long ago, a poor young man lived in a village. He had neither a house nor money, but he was very kind. One day, he went to a temple and prayed, “Please help me.” On his way home, he fell down and found a piece of straw.

The young man thought, “Perhaps this is the beginning of something,” and picked up the straw. Just then, a little insect flew over. He attached the insect to the straw and made a small toy. Looking at the toy he had made himself, he smiled.

After walking for a while, he saw a crying child. The mother was holding the child, but the child kept crying. When the young man showed the toy, the child smiled. The mother thanked him and gave him three delicious mandarins.

The young man continued walking with the mandarins. On the road, he met a thirsty woman. She said, “I have no water or food.” He gave her all his mandarins. The woman was delighted and gave him a beautiful piece of cloth.

The next day, the young man took the cloth to town. At the entrance, a horse could no longer move. Its owner was in trouble. The young man said, “I will give you this cloth, so please give me the horse.” The owner gave him the horse.

The young man gave the horse water to drink. Then he gently washed its body. Little by little, the horse recovered and began walking again. The young man was happy, but he thought, “I wish I had a house of my own.”

That evening, he met an old man in front of a large house. The old man was preparing to move to a distant town. He said, “Would you give me that horse? I will give you my house.” The young man was surprised but happily gave him the horse.

And so, starting with a single piece of straw, he received a large house. The villagers called him the Straw Millionaire. Even after becoming rich, he continued helping everyone. Every morning, he looked at a piece of straw and said, “Thank you.”
