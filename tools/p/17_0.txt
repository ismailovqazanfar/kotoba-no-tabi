Title: The Ant and the Grasshopper Prepare for Winter

Once upon a time, an ant and a grasshopper lived in a forest. All summer, the ant collected food every day. The grasshopper sang under a tree without working.

One day, the grasshopper said to the ant, “You don't have to work today. Shall we sing together?” Without stopping, the ant replied, “I must collect food by winter.”

The ant pointed to a large leaf. “Put your food on this. Please don't forget to prepare for winter.” But the grasshopper said, “There's no need to worry,” and sang again.

At last, winter came. There was no food in the grasshopper's house. Without eating breakfast, he went to the ant's home.

The ant opened the door. “Please don't stay outside. Come in quickly.” In a faint voice, the grasshopper said, “I must eat something. But I have no money.”

The ant brought some warm soup. “You don't have to pay. First, you must get warm.” The grasshopper drank the soup and thanked him again and again.

After the meal, the ant said, “You may stay here until spring. Please don't forget your songs.” The grasshopper was surprised, but that evening he sang a cheerful song for the ant's family.

The next summer, the grasshopper collected food too. Every day, he finished work by five, and then everyone sang together. That winter, the ant's home was full of both food and songs.
