Title: The Little Dragon and the Sky Flower

Long ago, a little dragon lived on top of a mountain. His name was Sora. Sora was not good at flying, but he loved helping people. Every morning, he visited the village and said, “Good morning,” to everyone.

One day, an old woman in the village was in trouble. Her precious clock was broken and had stopped working. Sora said, “I will fix it for you.” But he could not repair it by himself.

So Sora went to a little house in the forest. An old man who could repair anything lived there. The old man fixed the clock for him. Sora thanked the old man and brought him to the village.

The old woman made tea for both of them. Then she showed them a blue flower in her garden. “This is a sky flower. But it will soon lose its strength,” she explained. The flower needed clean water from the mountaintop.

Sora said, “I will bring you some water.” The old man gave him a large bottle. Sora ran toward the mountain, but the path was very long and dark. Even so, he kept walking for his friends.

At the top of the mountain, Sora found clean water. But on his way back, a strong wind began to blow. Sora tried to fly, but he could not fly well. Then some little birds came and showed him the way through the sky.

When Sora returned to the village, he watered the flower. Suddenly, it began to shine and grew into a large blue flower. Many little stars came out of it. The stars lit up the sky above the village.

The old woman gave Sora a little star. “This star is yours,” she said. Holding the star, Sora flew high for the first time. But he was so surprised that he fell onto a tree. Everyone laughed and helped him down.
