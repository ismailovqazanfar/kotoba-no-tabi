Title: The School of Little Powers

On top of a mountain stood a mysterious school. Every child there had a small special power. Some could fly a little, while others could hear voices from far away. But they could fly only one meter above the ground.

Yuki, a first-year student, could make a tiny light. But she could not make a bright one. Her friend Ren could hear not only the rain but also the sound of flowers opening. They practiced together every day.

One day, a large hole appeared in the schoolyard. A kitten was inside it. They could see the kitten, but the hole was too deep for them to rescue it. The teacher said, “Let us use everyone's powers.”

Ren listened carefully. He heard a faint voice from inside the hole. “The kitten is all right. But I can hear water, too,” Ren said. It began to rain, and water slowly entered the hole.

Yuki made a small light. The hole became a little brighter, and an old staircase appeared. But the stairs went only partway down. Yuki thought, “I wish I could make a little more light.”

Just then, Sora, who could fly only one meter high, arrived. He was carrying a long rope. “I cannot fly very high. But I can lower the rope,” he said. Everyone attached a small basket to its end.

Yuki made a light, and Ren listened to the kitten. Sora slowly lowered the basket. The kitten managed to climb inside. When everyone pulled the rope together, the kitten came out safely.

The next day, a new classroom was ready at the school. It was called “The Classroom of Little Powers.” The teacher said, “Great powers are not the only things that matter.” The kitten looked out of the classroom window and occasionally gave a tiny meow.

After that, the kitten became a favorite at school, too. It could neither fly nor make light. But it could make the children smile. Everyone laughed and said, “So the kitten has a special power, too!”
