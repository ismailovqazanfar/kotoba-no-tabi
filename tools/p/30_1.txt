Title: The Blue Train of Another World

In a certain town, there lived a girl named Haru. Every morning, Haru took a blue train to school. One day, she found a little white cat at the station. Her train was about to leave, but the cat was trembling in the rain.

Haru thought, “If I get on the train now, I probably won't be late. But I should help the cat.” Instead of boarding the train, she picked up the cat. At that moment, the station clock began turning backward. When Haru looked around, she was in an unfamiliar world.

In that world, there were two moons in the sky. The school stood above the clouds, and trains traveled through the air. Suddenly, the cat spoke. “This is the world where you chose a different path.” Haru was surprised, but she followed the cat.

The cat guided Haru to a large station. “If you want to return to your world, you should look for the blue train. But if night falls, the train might disappear,” it said. They hurried to search the station. However, there were only red trains.

Outside the station, Haru met another version of herself. That Haru wore a blue uniform and could fly. “I didn't help the cat that day. I caught the train, but I have regretted it ever since,” she said. Then she gave Haru a small blue ticket.

The two Harus went to the clock tower. The higher they climbed, the stronger the wind became. At the top stood a blue train. The other Haru said, “I wish I had helped the cat that day. That's why I want to help you now.”

Haru boarded the train. The cat tried to get on too, but the other Haru picked it up. She smiled and said, “I'll take good care of this little one. You don't need to worry.” As the train began moving, the two moons became one.

The next morning, Haru woke up at her familiar station. Although she had missed her train, the school clock still showed eight o'clock. There was a blue ticket in her pocket. She smiled, thinking that perhaps people sometimes visited mysterious worlds. On the back of the ticket, a tiny picture of a cat was glowing.
