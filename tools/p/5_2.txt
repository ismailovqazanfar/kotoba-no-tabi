Title: Grandma's Secret Destination

On Saturday morning, Kenta went to Grandma's house by bicycle. Her birthday was the following week. Last year, she had gone to the seaside with the family. This year, Kenta wanted to go on a trip with her again.

"Where are you going for your birthday? Shall we go to an island by boat?" But Grandma shook her head. "Thank you. But I'm not going anywhere next week." Kenta felt a little disappointed.

Just then, the telephone rang. "Yes, I'll go to the station at ten. We're taking the train today, right?" Grandma picked up a little bag. Kenta was puzzled. There were red shoes inside it.

"Grandma, where are you going today?" "Just to school for a little while. I'm going with my friends!" Kenta was even more puzzled. Grandma was seventy, but today she seemed like a student.

"I'll come with you." They walked to the station together. Three elderly men were waiting there. Each of their bags had red shoes inside too. The five of them took the train to the next town.

"When did you start going to school?" Kenta asked. "Last week!" "What kind of school is it?" Grandma smiled. "Well, you'll find the answer at school."

Music was playing in a room at the school. Grandma and her friends danced in their red shoes. Kenta was amazed. "Everyone is coming to my house next week. We're going to dance on my birthday!"

Kenta took the train home with Grandma too. "I'm not going anywhere on your birthday either. I'll dance with you." The next Saturday, Kenta came by bicycle again. Inside his bag was a little pair of red shoes.
