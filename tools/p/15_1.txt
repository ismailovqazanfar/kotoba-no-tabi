Title: The White Friend of Sakura Dormitory

In spring, Ren arrived at Sakura Dormitory. Ten students lived there. At the entrance was a notice: “Please put tea on the kitchen table at ten at night.”

The woman who looked after the dormitory explained three rules. “You must not close the window at night. You must not touch the white hat. And you may drink the tea, but leave one cup.”

That night, Ren put tea on the table. A small white ghost came in through the window. “May I sit beside you?” the ghost asked politely.

The ghost’s name was Shiro. “I live in this dormitory. I know everyone’s name, too.” Every night, Shiro sewed buttons onto the students’ clothes. His white hat was a gift from his mother.

The next evening was very cold. Ren forgot the rule and closed the window. He put out the tea at ten, but Shiro did not come. A button lay on the hallway floor.

Ren searched for Shiro. A small voice came from outside the window. “Excuse me. May I open it?” Shiro was outside. His hat was caught in the window.

Ren hurried to open the window. “I’m sorry. May I touch your hat?” “You may now,” Shiro replied. Ren freed the hat and handed it to him.

“I can go through walls. But this hat can’t.” Shiro laughed. The next day, Ren sewed a little button onto the hat. Shiro still wears his precious hat today.
