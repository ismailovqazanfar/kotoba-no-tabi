Title: The Contest Between the North Wind and the Sun

Long ago, the North Wind and the Sun lived in the sky. The North Wind was very strong and always spoke loudly. The Sun was quiet and always smiled. One day, the North Wind said, “I think I am stronger.”

The Sun said, “Really? Then let's have a contest.” Just then, a man was walking along a road. He was wearing a thick coat. The North Wind said, “Whoever makes that man take off his coat wins, right?”

The North Wind went first. “I'll show you an amazing wind,” he said, and blew hard. The trees beside the road swayed wildly. The man said, “It's cold. I must be careful...” Then he held his coat tightly.

The North Wind blew even harder. But the man did not take off his coat. The man thought, “I wonder when this wind will stop.” The North Wind became tired. “I'll stop now. Sun, it's your turn,” he said.

The Sun slowly appeared from behind the clouds. The sky became bright. The man said, “Ah, the weather has become lovely!” The Sun said softly, “I think warm days feel wonderful.”

There was a small park near the road. A flower festival was being held there. The man decided to rest for a while. “I don't need my coat anymore,” he said, taking it off. Then he drank tea beneath a tree.

The North Wind was astonished. “I lost. The Sun won,” he said. The Sun replied, “I think strength alone is not enough.” The North Wind answered, “That's true. Gentleness can be useful too.”

That evening, there was a little celebration in the sky. The North Wind said, “I think I'll blow gently tomorrow.” But the next morning, he shouted, “Good morning!” in his booming voice again. Everyone in town woke up. The Sun laughed and said, “What an energetic wind!”
