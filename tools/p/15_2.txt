Title: Grandpa’s Secret Job

Mina lived with her father, mother, and grandfather. Her father worked at city hall. Her mother was a dentist. But she was not quite sure what her grandfather did.

Every morning, Grandpa went into a small room. Clothing catalogs and old photographs lay on his desk. He kept many little boxes on a shelf. Mina had never been inside that room.

One day, the children talked about their families’ jobs at school. Mina could not explain only her grandfather’s work. When she got home, she asked, “Grandpa, may I watch you work?”

Grandpa smiled and opened the door. “You may come in. But you must not open this box.” Mina looked at the desk. There was a red outfit and a very tiny pair of shoes.

“Do you make baby clothes?” Grandpa shook his head. “I make clothes for dolls. I sell them at the shop in front of the station.” For the first time, Mina learned what her grandfather did.

Mina asked, “May I make something, too?” Grandpa gave her a small piece of cloth. “You must not put the needle on the floor. Please put it in this box.” Together, they attached a white flower to the red outfit.

The next day was Mina’s birthday. Grandpa gave her the box. Inside was an old doll. It was wearing yesterday’s red outfit and the tiny shoes.

Her mother said, “I know this doll. It’s mine!” Grandpa replied, “Now it’s Mina’s doll.” Mina hugged the doll and laughed. “You may play with us too, Mom!”
