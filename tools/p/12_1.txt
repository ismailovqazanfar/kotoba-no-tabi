Title: The Final Baton

One autumn morning, a small school held a sports festival. It had been rainy yesterday, but today was sunny. The schoolyard was lively, and the breeze was cool.

Ren from the red team and Sora from the white team were always rivals. Last year, Sora had been faster. But this year, Ren was faster than before, and he enjoyed his morning practice.

Before the relay, a little child asked them, “Who is faster, Ren or Sora?” Both answered together, “I’m faster.” The little child was puzzled.

The teacher took two batons out of a box. One was thick, and the other was thin. Ren chose a light one, but Sora’s weighed just as much. They looked at each other and smiled a little.

At last, the relay began. Ren and Sora were the final runners. “Who is the fastest on the red team?” asked the same child. Everyone shouted, “Ren!”

The white team reached the final lap a little before the red team. But the runner before Sora fell. The baton dropped at Sora’s feet. Before picking it up, Sora took the child’s hand.

Ren finished first, but he went straight over to Sora. The child’s injury was not serious. Sora had not come first, but the applause for him was louder than it had been for Ren.

On the way home, the little child asked them again, “Who is the strongest in the school?” Ren pointed to Sora, and Sora pointed to Ren. This time their answers were different, but both looked happier than they had that morning.
