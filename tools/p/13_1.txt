Title: The Little Shop in the Cloud Town

One weekend, a boy named Ren went to the library to borrow a book. He opened a blue book. Suddenly, everything around him turned white. A moment later, Ren was in a town above the clouds.

A little dragon came up to Ren. “Where would you like to go? I'll show you the way.” Ren was at a loss. “I want to go home. Is there some way?” The dragon pointed to an old station.

A ticket home cost ten star coins. Ren did not have a single one. “I want a job. I want to work somewhere.” Beside the station stood a narrow, empty shop.

The dragon lent Ren the shop. Ren cleaned it. “I want to open a shop here. Please order anything you like.” But there was nothing on the shelves.

The Cloud King was the first to arrive. “I want something interesting.” Ren had three sheets of paper in his bag. He made a paper airplane. The king's eyes opened wide.

The next day, the townspeople came to buy paper airplanes. “I want one too.” “I want to play with my child.” Everyone flew airplanes in the spacious square. A long line formed outside Ren's shop.

By evening, Ren had ten star coins. He went to the station to buy a ticket. The dragon came too. “I want to come back here to play. When would be good?” “Any time is fine.”

Ren returned to his own world on a glowing train. There was a small note in his bag. “An order: one hundred paper airplanes. From the Cloud King.” Ren laughed. Now he had a weekend job.
