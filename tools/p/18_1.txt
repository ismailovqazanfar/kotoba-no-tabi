Title: A Sky Ride with a Little Dragon

In a town, there lived a girl named Mio. A little blue dragon lived at Mio's house. His name was Sora. Mio's hobby was riding Sora through the sky.

Sora was still a baby. But he could fly a little. Every morning before school, Mio practiced flying with Sora. Sora especially loved flying near the clouds.

One day, the town held a sky festival. Mio really wanted to enter the festival with Sora. But Sora could not fly high. No matter how many times they practiced, he struggled to gain height.

The day before the festival, Mio looked up information about dragons on the internet. She learned that little dragons could fly high on windy days. Mio wrote this in her diary. Then she said to Sora, “Let's fly together tomorrow, okay?”

The next morning, a strong wind was blowing. Before riding Sora, Mio put on her blue jacket. Then she climbed onto Sora's back. Riding the wind, Sora flew higher and higher.

The two flew above the great clouds. There were mountains of white clouds up there. Mio enjoyed watching little birds above the clouds. But suddenly, the wind stopped. Sora began to fall slowly.

Mio became frightened. But she saw Sora's little wings moving. “Sora, you can fly!” she said. Sora flapped his wings with all his strength. And he managed to fly again.

The two returned to the festival square. Everyone saw the little dragon and applauded loudly. Mio said, “My hobby is riding a dragon. But I wonder what Sora's hobby is.” Just then, Sora fell asleep inside Mio's bag.
