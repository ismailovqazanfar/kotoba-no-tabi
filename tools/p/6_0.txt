Title: Momotaro and the Island of Ogres

Long ago, an old man and an old woman lived near a mountain. One day, the old woman washed clothes in the river. A huge peach was floating on the water.

At home, the old woman cut open the peach. Inside was a little boy. His name was Momotaro. He ate rice and fish every day and grew big.

One day, Momotaro heard a story about ogres in the village. The ogres had taken the village treasure to their island. “What will you do on the island?” asked the old man. “I will talk to the ogres,” said Momotaro.

The old woman made millet dumplings. On the mountain path, Momotaro met a dog, a monkey, and a pheasant. “Would you like to come to the island with me? Let's have some dumplings,” he said. The three animals ate the dumplings.

They went to the island by boat. In front of the gate, they heard a loud noise. “What was that?” asked Momotaro. It was an ogre's stomach rumbling.

The ogre was in front of the treasure chest. “I didn't eat breakfast,” he said. Momotaro looked in his bag. Only one dumpling was left.

“Would you like to eat with us in the village?” asked Momotaro. “Let's go to the village. Please take the treasure, too,” said the ogre. Everyone boarded the boat with the treasure chest.

In the village, everyone cooked. The ogre ate ten bowls of rice, but nobody minded. The next day, he worked in a large field. This time, he carried vegetables instead of treasure.
