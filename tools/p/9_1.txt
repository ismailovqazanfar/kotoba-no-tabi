Title: The Clumsy After-School Band

A small school had a band with three members. Haru played guitar, Mina played the drum, and Sora sang. All three loved music, but they were not very good at performing.

There was a school concert scheduled for Saturday. The three friends practiced a lot every day. “We have time, so let’s practice today too.” Haru always brought his big guitar.

But Haru could understand only a little written music. Mina drummed very fast, while Sora sang very slowly. “Why aren’t we together?” All three tilted their heads in puzzlement.

On Friday, Sora spoke in a small voice. “I am bad at singing, so I won’t sing tomorrow.” Mina looked sad too. “I don’t like this difficult song either.”

Just then, a little first grader came in. “What kind of music do you like?” Haru asked. “The school lunch song, because I understand the words really well.” It was a song all three friends knew well too.

“This song is easy, so let’s sing it tomorrow.” Sora wrote the song on paper in large letters. The first grader could not read kanji, so his copy was entirely in hiragana.

On Saturday, the three friends went onstage. Their playing was still a little clumsy, but everyone sang along. Sora’s voice could not be heard at all. Everyone else was singing so loudly.

After the song, the principal rubbed his stomach. “Why am I hungry?” The first grader laughed. “Because you love school lunch too!” For the first time, the three friends stood tall with pride.
