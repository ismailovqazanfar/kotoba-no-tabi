Title: Dad's Evening Exam

Yu's father worked at a bank. He got up at six thirty every morning. From Monday to Friday, he worked from nine in the morning until five in the afternoon. But one week, he began coming home late every evening.

On Tuesday evening, Yu asked his mother, "What time is it now?" "It is eight ten." His father's chair was empty. Yu's plate and his father's plate held the same fish dish.

His father came home at nine. "I studied at the library yesterday and today. I have an exam on Sunday." Yu was surprised. His father was not a student.

On Saturday too, his father got up at seven. "Are you working today?" Yu asked. "No, the bank is closed. But I will study this morning." On the desk were a book and a little clock.

On Sunday morning, his father did not study. He did not open a book at lunchtime either. "What time does the exam start and finish?" "From eight to eight thirty this evening." It was a very late exam.

Evening came. But his father was still at home. "It is seven fifty now. It is nearly time for your exam." Yu was worried. His father took a big book and the clock into Yu's room.

You are the teacher, Yu. My exam is telling you a story. You have trouble falling asleep every night, don't you?" At eight, his father began the story. It was about a little bear and the moon. The bear had a very funny voice.

At eight ten, the story ended. Yu was already dreaming. His mother whispered, "It is eight ten now. Your exam was a great success, wasn't it?" But his father was already asleep too.
