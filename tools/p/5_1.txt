Title: The Night Train to the Stars

There was a little station at the edge of town. Yui went to school by bicycle every day, but she never went to that station. One night, a blue train arrived there. Behind its windows were lots of little stars.

Yui went to the station alone. A boy in a white cap stood in front of the train. "Where does this train go?" "It goes to the school for stars. I'm Sora. Let's go together."

Yui went up into the sky by train with Sora. The town and mountains already looked tiny. "Do stars go to school by train too?" "Yes. Their school is on the other side of the moon!" The stars on the seats twinkled.

But the train stopped in front of the moon. Sora looked into the dark sky. "The next station's light is out. Where could the station be?" The stars looked out of the windows too, but there was no station in sight.

The smallest star was beside Yui. "I'm not going to school. I'm not going anywhere. My light is too small." Yui looked at the little star. Its light was yellow and warm.

"Let's go to the front together. Your light matters too!" Yui went to the driver's cab with the little star. In its yellow light, the station's name became visible. "Ah, here it is!" Sora slowly moved the train forward.

The stars walked from the station to school. The little star went with its friends too. "When are you going home, Yui?" Sora asked. "Well, let's see. I have breakfast with my mother at six in the morning."

Yui returned to town on the blue train. In the morning, there was a little ticket in her pocket. It said, "Next time, with your mother." That night, the yellow star in the sky twinkled twice.
