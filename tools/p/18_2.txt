Title: The Section Chief's Secret Hobby

At a company, there was a section chief named Tanaka. Chief Tanaka was always quiet and rarely smiled. The employees did not know his hobby. They thought his hobby was working every day.

One Friday, there was a small party at the company. The department manager asked everyone, “What is your hobby?” Sato said, “My hobby is playing the piano.” Yamamoto answered, “I like collecting old stamps.”

Then it was Chief Tanaka's turn. His face turned a little red as he said, “My hobby is singing songs.” Everyone exclaimed, “Really!” in surprise. “Please sing for us!” said the department manager. But the chief hesitated and could not bring himself to sing.

The next morning, Sato went shopping. In front of the station, he saw a man in red clothes. The man was singing loudly. When Sato looked closely, it was Chief Tanaka. Sato was so surprised that he forgot to do his shopping.

The chief was also wearing a red hat. Little children were listening to his songs and laughing. Before singing, he always changed his hat. When he put on the red hat, he could sing cheerfully.

On Monday, Sato met the chief at the office. “You were singing in front of the station on Saturday, weren't you?” he said. The chief looked very embarrassed. But Sato asked, “Can I sing with you, too?” For the first time, the chief laughed heartily.

One week later, everyone from the company gathered in front of the station. Sato played the piano, and Yamamoto sang. The department manager and even the company president sang along. Chief Tanaka had a wonderful time singing with everyone.

The following Friday, the company president announced a new rule. “Every Friday, let's all sing one song before work.” Everyone was delighted. But Chief Tanaka was a little troubled. Now he had to bring his red hat to work every week.
