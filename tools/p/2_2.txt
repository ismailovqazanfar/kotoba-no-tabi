Title: Dad's Black Bag

On Sunday morning, Yui was in the kitchen with her dad. There were two black bags on the table. One was her dad's work bag, and the other was her grandfather's old bag.

Dad looked at the clock and hurried to his feet. “Are you going to work today too?” Yui asked. “Yes. I'll be home at lunchtime,” he answered, taking one of the bags and leaving.

Yui looked at the bag that was left. “Is this Grandpa's?” she asked her mother. Her mother answered, “Yes, it is,” so Yui carried the bag to Grandpa's room.

Grandpa opened the bag and tilted his head. “Neither this notebook nor that English dictionary is mine,” he said. There was also a key to the office inside, and Yui gave a little cry of “Oh!”

Yui phoned her dad. “Dad, are there books in your bag, or sweets?” she asked. “Chocolate and a little car. What is this bag?” Dad was puzzled too.

Yui and Grandpa went to Dad's office. Dad was standing near the entrance. “That's Dad's car. He's in front of that car,” Yui said, and Grandpa waved.

After they exchanged the bags, Yui asked, “Grandpa, are those sweets yours?” “No, they aren't. These are presents I brought for you,” Grandpa answered. “Oh, I see,” Yui said with a smile.

Dad laughed too, but Grandpa suddenly looked inside his bag. The little car was missing. On Dad's desk, someone from the office was rolling it back and forth, saying, “What a nice car!”
