Title: The Three Little Pigs and Their New Houses

Once upon a time, three little pigs lived near a forest. One spring day, their mother said, “You're all grown up now. Let's build your own houses.” The three pigs were thinking of building their houses before summer.

The oldest pig intended to build a straw house. He thought, “I'll finish quickly and play a lot.” The middle pig planned to build a wooden house. The youngest pig was thinking of building a strong house.

The two older pigs built their houses quickly. But the youngest had not finished his house yet. Every day, he carried stones. His brothers laughed and asked, “Still not finished?” But the youngest had decided not to rush.

One day, a big wolf came out of the forest. The wolf was hungry and wanted the pigs' food. He tried to open the straw house's door, but it would not open. So he decided to blow hard.

The straw house fell apart immediately. The oldest pig ran to the wooden house. But when the wolf blew again, the wooden house fell apart too. The two pigs decided to escape to their youngest brother's house.

The stone house was already finished. The wolf tried to knock it down, but he could not. He grew tired and looked ready to cry. The youngest pig asked through the window, “Are you hungry?”

The pigs decided to give the wolf some warm soup. He was surprised, but ate it happily. Then he decided never to destroy houses again. Together, they also made plans to plant flowers in the garden.

When summer came, the three pigs lived happily in the stone house. It was also arranged that the wolf would visit every week. One day, the wolf said, “This time, I'll build my own house.” But he had not carried even one stone yet.
