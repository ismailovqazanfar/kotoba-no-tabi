Title: Hoshimaru the Robot and the School Secret

In a town, there was a little robot named Hoshimaru. He had been created by a scientist in the town. One spring day, Hoshimaru was enrolled in a school for humans. He worried about whether he could make friends.

On his first day at school, the teacher called Hoshimaru's name. When he stood up, a small light shone from his head. The children laughed at him, and his face glowed red. But the teacher praised him, saying, “What wonderful energy!”

The next day, during math class, Hoshimaru solved a difficult problem immediately. The teacher praised him, saying, “Excellent!” His classmates also said, “You're really smart!” The star on Hoshimaru's chest glowed gold.

However, during lunch break, he made a big mistake. While trying to help a friend, he sprayed water all over the classroom. The teacher scolded him severely. Some children were upset because their notebooks had been soaked.

Hoshimaru sat quietly at his desk, trying to make himself small. Mina, who sat beside him, asked, “What's wrong?” Hoshimaru answered, “I'm a useless robot.” Mina held his hand and said, “That's not true.”

After school that day, the school's big clock stopped. It was a precious clock made a hundred years earlier. The teachers did not know what to do. Encouraged by Mina, Hoshimaru went over to the clock.

Hoshimaru examined the inside of the clock. One small screw had come loose. He fixed the screw with his finger. Then the clock started moving, and a beautiful chime rang throughout the school.

Hoshimaru was praised by both the teacher and his friends. The teacher said, “You worked very hard today.” Hoshimaru was so happy that rainbow-colored light shone from his head. Everyone laughed again, but this time Hoshimaru laughed with them.
