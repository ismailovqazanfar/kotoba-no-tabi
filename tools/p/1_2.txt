Title: Yuki and the Star Hospital

Yuki was a ten-year-old student. One winter night, on her way home, a little star fell from the sky. It flashed just once on her red glove.

The star's light was about to go out. When Yuki cupped it in both hands, a silver path appeared at her feet. At the end of the path stood a little hospital, bright even at night.

On the hospital door was written “Star Hospital.” A cat in white clothes came out. “Nice to meet you. I'm Mimi. I'm the doctor of this hospital,” said the cat.

Yuki laid the star on a little bed. Doctor Mimi looked at it carefully and said, “This star is still only one year old.” The star was afraid to go back to the sky alone and was trembling a little.

Yuki sat down by the bed. “I'm scared of being alone too,” she said, and put her red glove next to the star. The star crept into the glove and shone brighter than before.

When Doctor Mimi opened the window, two stars came down from the sky. Each one took hold of one end of the red glove. Still inside the glove, the little star rose into the sky together with them.

The next night, when Yuki looked at the sky, three stars stood in a row. Only the one in the middle was glowing red. Yuki put on her other red glove and waved to the sky.
