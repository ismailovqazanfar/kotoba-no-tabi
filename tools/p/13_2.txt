Title: Separate Bills

On Sunday morning, Mina talked with her mother in the kitchen. “I want to go somewhere today.” Her mother nodded. “I want some new shoes. Let's go shopping near the station.”

There was a little purse in Mina's bag. Inside was one thousand yen of pocket money. Tomorrow was her mother's birthday. “I want to buy her a present,” Mina said softly.

They went to a shoe shop to look at shoes. “What colour would you prefer?” the assistant asked. Her mother answered, “Any colour is fine. I want light shoes.” But all the shoes were expensive.

By noon, they were tired. Mina was thirsty. “I want something to drink.” Her mother was hungry too. They went to a little café to have a meal.

The waiter brought some water. “May I take your order?” “Two sandwiches and two teas, please,” her mother said. Mina looked at a little cake in the corner of the menu.

Her mother went to wash her hands. Mina spoke quietly to the waiter. “I'd like that cake too. Separate bills, please.” The waiter looked at Mina's little purse. Then he smiled.

After the meal, two cakes arrived. Both Mina and her mother were surprised. Her mother laughed. “Actually, I ordered a cake too. I wanted to treat you today.”

“And I wanted to treat you, Mum. It's for your birthday!” Mina said. “Then let's have one each.” “Let's do that.” They paid their bills separately, but they were equally happy.
