Title: The Apartment Grandma Was Looking For

In a certain town, there was a girl named Hana. Hana's grandmother had lived in the countryside for a long time. One day, Grandma said, “I want to look for a new apartment in town.” “Shall we help you look?” Hana asked. Grandma smiled happily.

The next day, Hana asked what kind of apartment Grandma wanted to live in. “I'd like a room with a big window,” Grandma said. “And a home close to the station would be nice,” she added. Hana wrote down the details of the apartment Grandma wanted in her notebook.

They made an appointment to visit the real estate agency on Saturday. But it had been raining since morning. Grandma put on a warm coat and a hat. Hana wore a red sweater. “Well then, let's go!” Grandma said cheerfully.

At the real estate agency, there was a kind man wearing glasses. He showed them three apartments. The first one was new and clean. But it had small windows. The second apartment was near the station, but it was very dark.

The third apartment was on the ground floor of an old building. It had a large window and a small garden. Many flowers were blooming in the garden. Grandma looked out the window and smiled. “This place looks a little like the village where I was born,” she said.

“I think this apartment is the best,” Hana said. But Grandma didn't answer right away. “Hmm, I still have something to do today,” she said. Then she added, “Let's make an appointment to come here again on Sunday afternoon.” Hana couldn't understand why Grandma wanted to return.

On Sunday, Hana and Grandma returned to the apartment. This time, a little table stood in the garden. On the table was Hana's favorite cake. Nearby stood Grandpa, wearing a suit, along with Hana's father and mother. Everyone was smiling.

“Happy birthday, Hana!” everyone called out together. Grandma smiled. “Actually, I was looking for this apartment for you,” she said. She had wanted a place where the whole family could gather. Hana was so surprised and happy that she cried. From then on, the little garden became a treasured place where the family often met.
