Title: The Heavenly Maiden's Feather Robe

Long, long ago, there was a small village near the sea. A young man named Taro lived there. Taro was good at catching fish, but he could not fly. Every day, he looked at the blue sky and envied the birds.

One morning, Taro went to a pine tree near the sea. Suddenly, he heard a beautiful song. Beneath the tree lay a lovely feather robe. In the sea, a heavenly maiden was swimming happily.

Taro picked up the feather robe. Then the heavenly maiden came out of the sea. She said, “That is my feather robe. Without it, I cannot return to the sky.” Taro was surprised and gave it back.

But the heavenly maiden did not fly away immediately. “To thank you, I will show you a heavenly dance,” she said. Taro happily asked, “Will I really be able to see a dance from the sky?” The maiden put on her robe and began to dance slowly.

As the feather robe moved in the wind, beautiful sounds could be heard. Taro could see not only the heavenly maiden but also little lights. The lights circled through the sky like flowers. Taro was speechless.

Before long, the heavenly maiden rose into the sky. Taro waved, but he could no longer see her face. Her song could still be heard from far away. Only one white feather remained beneath the pine tree.

Taro took the feather back to the village. The village children wanted to make a bird that could fly. Taro made a little wooden bird, but it could not fly. The children laughed and tried making another one.

After a few days, a large bird-shaped toy was ready. When the wind blew, the bird rose into the sky. Taro and the children laughed loudly. From that day on, the villagers treasured not only flying toys but also beautiful songs.
