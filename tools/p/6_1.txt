Title: The Cooking Club's Little Guest

At a high school, there was a small cooking club. Its only members were Mina and Ren. On Friday afternoon, they read a cookbook in the kitchen. “What shall we do today?” asked Ren.

“Shall we make an egg dish?” suggested Mina. Just then, a faint sound came from the cupboard. Ren opened it. A tiny white spirit was licking a plate.

“What is that?” asked Mina. “My dinner,” said the spirit. But there was nothing on the plate. The two students looked at each other.

“Would you like to eat here with us?” asked Ren. Mina checked the fridge. There was no meat or fish. “Let's cook with eggs and vegetables,” they decided.

Ren chopped the vegetables. Mina made an omelet filled with rice. The spirit drank water at the table. But the water passed through its body and fell onto the floor.

The students wiped up the water with a cloth. Then they put the rice omelet on the table. The spirit took a bite. Its white body turned yellow, just like the egg.

“What will you eat next?” asked Mina. The spirit ate a red tomato. This time, its body turned red. Ren laughed and wrote “A guest who changes color” in his notebook.

“Let's cook here again next week,” said Mina. The spirit smiled for the first time. On Monday, they took a cooking club photograph. Between the two students, the yellow spirit appeared in the picture too.
