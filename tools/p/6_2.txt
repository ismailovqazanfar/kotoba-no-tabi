Title: A Park Photo and Three Bread Rolls

On Saturday morning, Yui ate breakfast at home. It was her usual bread and egg. Every Saturday, she watched television alone. But that day, she called her friend Sora.

“Would you like to take photos in the park today?” asked Yui. “That sounds nice. Let's have lunch too,” said Sora. They met in front of the bakery at twelve.

Sora bought three bread rolls at the shop. Yui bought water and fruit. “Three?” asked Yui. “One is for our guest,” said Sora.

In the park, they took pictures of flowers. Sora kept checking his watch. “What time is our guest coming?” asked Yui. “At one,” said Sora, but even at half past one, nobody had arrived.

“What shall we do?” asked Yui. They each ate a bread roll on a bench. The third roll stayed in the bag. Just then, a small sound came from behind a tree.

“What is that sound?” asked Yui. A huge rabbit came out from behind the tree. It was carrying a red bag. Yui was so surprised that she dropped the bread bag.

The rabbit took off its huge costume head. Inside was Sora's older sister. That morning, she had worked as a rabbit mascot at a department store. “I forgot my change of clothes,” she said with a laugh.

“Let's take a photo together,” said Yui. An elderly man nearby took a picture of all three. That evening, Yui looked at the photo at home. This had been no ordinary Saturday, but a day with a giant rabbit.
