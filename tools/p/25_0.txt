Title: The Magic Mill That Grinds Salt

Long, long ago, a poor older brother and a rich younger brother lived in a village near the sea. The older brother worked hard every day, but he never had enough money. One day, he met a little old man in the mountains. The old man gave him a magic stone mill.

The old man explained, “If you turn it to the right, it will produce whatever you want. When you want to stop it, turn it to the left.” By the time the older brother got home, it was already night. He said, “I want rice,” and turned the mill to the right. Then lots of white rice came out.

The older brother turned the mill to the left and stopped it. Then he made food and clothes too. Even after becoming rich, he remained kind. Whenever someone in the village was hungry, he gave them food. Everyone was very happy.

When the younger brother heard about the mill, he wanted it for himself. “If I get that mill, I'll make hundreds of millions,” he thought. One night, he entered his older brother's house and took the mill. But he did not know how to stop it.

The younger brother took the mill and sailed out to sea in a boat. There was food on the boat, but there was no salt. He said, “I want salt,” and turned the mill to the right. Then white salt began pouring out without stopping.

Even when the younger brother tried turning it to the left, he could not stop the mill. He did not know the correct way to stop it. There was far too much salt, and he could do nothing. The boat became heavier and heavier. “Help!” he shouted loudly.

At last, the boat sank into the sea. The younger brother swam to a nearby island. But the mill fell to the bottom of the sea. Crying, he said, “There are things more important than money.”

Even now, the mill is producing salt at the bottom of the sea. Fish can stare at it and octopuses can touch it, but it never stops. People say that this mill is why seawater is salty. If you ever go to the sea, listen carefully to the waves. You might hear the sound of the mill.
