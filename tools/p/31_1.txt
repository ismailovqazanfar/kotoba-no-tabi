Title: The Fox Girl and the Flying Lunch Box

In a small town on a mountain lived a fox girl named Yuzu. She had big ears and a fluffy tail. She could even smell food from far away. The townspeople said that Yuzu apparently had the best nose in the world.

One morning, Yuzu stopped on her way to school. “Oh, what a wonderful smell of bread! It must be delicious!” she thought. But there was no bakery nearby. The smell seemed to be coming from the sky.

When Yuzu looked up, she saw a little box floating in the blue sky. It had two white wings and was flying like a bird. “That looks like a lunch box. But why is it flying?” Yuzu wondered. The box looked as if it might fly away over the mountain.

Yuzu chased the box all the way to the old clock tower. There she found a little tanuki inventor. “I made that box,” the tanuki said. “It was supposed to carry lunch to the mountain hospital.” But apparently the box had taken the wrong route.

“The children at the hospital must be hungry by now,” the tanuki said worriedly. Yuzu lifted her nose and carefully followed the scent. “It seems there's rolled omelet and sweet bread inside that lunch box,” she said. Together they followed the smell into the forest.

A strong wind was blowing through the forest. The box was caught in a tall tree and looked ready to fall at any moment. Yuzu climbed up and caught it with her tail. The food inside was a little squashed, but it still looked delicious.

When the two reached the hospital, the children smiled happily. A nurse said, “I've heard today's lunch is especially delicious.” As everyone began eating, Yuzu's stomach made a loud rumbling sound. The children laughed and shared their bread with her.

The next day, the tanuki built a new flying box. This time, he painted a big nose on it. “They say this box can find its way by smell,” he boasted. But instead of flying to the hospital, the box went straight to the town's cake shop. “It's just like me!” Yuzu said, bursting into laughter.
