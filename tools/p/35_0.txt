Title: Puss in Boots and the King

Once upon a time, a poor young man lived in a village. When his father died, the young man inherited a cat. The cat could speak like a human. One day, the cat put on a pair of long boots and said, “Master, please leave everything to me.”

The cat caught a large rabbit in the forest. He took it to the castle. At the gate, he said, “I would like to meet His Majesty.” The guard was surprised but showed the cat to the king's room.

The king looked at the cat and said, “Who are you?” The cat bowed deeply. “I am a messenger of the Marquis of Carabas. I have brought this gift for Your Majesty,” he said humbly. The king was very pleased.

The next day, the cat took the young man to a river. “Please swim here. I shall take care of the rest,” he said. Just then, the king's carriage passed nearby. The cat shouted, “Help! Someone has stolen the marquis's clothes!”

The king stopped his carriage. “What happened?” he asked. The cat replied, “My master is in trouble.” The king gave the young man fine clothes and invited him into the carriage.

The cat ran ahead of the carriage to a great castle. A wizard lived there. The cat politely asked, “Could you turn yourself into a little mouse?” The wizard smiled proudly and became a mouse. The cat immediately caught him.

Before long, the king's carriage reached the castle. The cat was waiting at the gate. “Welcome, Your Majesty. This is the castle of the Marquis of Carabas,” he announced respectfully. The young man was astonished but said nothing. The king and princess were amazed by the beautiful castle.

The king took a liking to the young man and allowed him to marry the princess. On the wedding day, the cat wore golden boots. When the young man said, “Thank you,” the cat replied, “You are most welcome, Master.” Then he whispered, “Now I would like some fish, please.” Everyone burst out laughing, and they lived happily ever after.
