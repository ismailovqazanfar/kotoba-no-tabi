Title: Dad's Quiet Sunday

On Sunday morning, Dad had a fever. He was usually full of energy, but that day he did not even eat breakfast. His daughter Hana sat beside his bed, worried.

Mom went to the hospital with Dad. The doctor said, “Please don't go out today. You must get plenty of rest.” Dad no longer had to do any work.

At home, Mom put the medicine on the desk. “You must take this medicine after meals. Please don't forget.” Dad took off his jacket and got back into bed.

Mom went shopping. “I'll be back by twelve. Hana, please don't wake Dad.” Without turning on the television, Hana brought some paper and a pencil.

Hana wrote “Please be quiet” in large letters and put the paper on the door. Then, without making a sound, she lined up her stuffed animals in the hallway. Their dog Pochi sat beside them too.

At eleven, Dad woke up. Looking into the hallway, he asked, “Is everyone ill too?” Hana replied, “You don't need to worry. But you must wait your turn.”

Hana looked at her toy clock. “Mr. Bear must take his medicine by half past eleven. Pochi doesn't have to take any.” Dad smiled and sat down behind the bear.

Mom returned by twelve. Hana said, “This is a hospital. You don't have to pay.” Dad thanked the little doctor and ate every bit of his lunch.
