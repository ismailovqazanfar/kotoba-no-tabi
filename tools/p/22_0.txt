Title: The Tanuki and the Spinning Wheel

Long, long ago, there was a small house near a mountain. An old man and an old woman lived there. Every evening, the old woman made thread with a spinning wheel. The thread she made was very beautiful.

One cold night, the old woman heard a little sound outside the window. A brown tanuki was sitting beneath it. The tanuki was staring at the spinning wheel the old woman was using. Then it began moving its front paws in circles to the sound of the wheel.

“Oh, what a cute tanuki!” the old woman said with a smile. But the old man was a little worried. “If the tanuki comes inside, our food might disappear,” he said. “This little one isn't a bad tanuki,” the old woman replied.

The next morning, the old woman had something to do in town. The old man went to the mountain too. They were due to return in the evening. The old woman closed the windows and left the house. But one small window remained open.

When the old woman returned that evening, she heard the spinning wheel inside the house. “Hmm, has the old man come home already?” she wondered. She quietly opened the door and saw the tanuki sitting in front of the wheel. With its tiny front paws, it was working hard to spin thread.

The thread the tanuki had made was long and beautiful. The old woman was surprised. “You're better at this than I am!” she said. The tanuki spun around happily. But it spun so much that it became tangled in the thread.

“Shall I help you now?” the old woman asked. The tanuki bowed its little head. The old woman put on her glasses and carefully removed the thread, little by little. At last, the tanuki was free and licked her hand.

That night, the old man looked at the thread the tanuki had made. “This is wonderful. Let's help too, starting tomorrow,” he said. From then on, the tanuki spun thread with the old woman every day. The villagers wore warm coats and hats made from that thread. The tanuki became the most famous spinning teacher in the village.
