Title: The Little Ninja and the Sleepy Teacher

Long ago, there was a small ninja school in the mountains. A boy named Taro lived there. Taro wanted to become a strong ninja, but he was not very fast at running. His teacher, Kuroda, always looked a little sleepy.

One day, the teacher began ninja training on the mountain. The students climbed trees and jumped across rocks. Taro had climbed a tree once before, but this was his first time with such a tall tree. When he looked up, his legs felt a little weak.

Taro woke up early every day and practiced. He ran, jumped, and carried heavy stones. Little by little, he became able to run faster. But every evening, he became very sleepy.

One morning, the teacher announced loudly, “Today is a special ninja exam!” The students changed into black clothes and went into the forest. For the exam, they had to take a red flag without being seen by the teacher. Taro's heart was beating fast.

Taro hid behind trees and walked quietly. But the teacher was sleeping under a large tree. The red flag was resting on the teacher's head. Taro was surprised, but he quietly took the flag.

Just then, the teacher sneezed loudly. “Achoo!” Taro was startled and fell from the tree. But he did not let go of the flag. The teacher opened his eyes and said, “Oh, amazing!”

The teacher saw the flag in Taro's hand and became happy. “Well done. You're a strong ninja now!” Taro blushed. The other students gathered, and everyone drank tea and ate sweets together.

The next day, the teacher fell asleep during training again. Taro placed a small sign beside him. The sign said, “The Sleepiest Ninja in the World.” Everyone laughed loudly, and the school became a very lively place.
