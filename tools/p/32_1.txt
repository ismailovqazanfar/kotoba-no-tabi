Title: The Magic Broom and the Letter from the Sky

Long ago, in a small town on a mountain, there lived a girl named Mio. In that town, some people flew through the sky on magic brooms. Mio bought an old broom so she could fly too. But the broom did not move at all.

Mio practiced every morning so that she could learn to fly. She sat on the broom exactly as the magic book instructed. But the broom only ran along the ground. “Why can't I fly?” Mio asked the sky.

One day, a small blue bird flew in through the window. It brought a letter from a place called the Sky School. The letter said, “Come to the eastern mountain tomorrow morning.” Mio checked many times whether the letter was real.

The next morning, Mio went to the mountain as the letter instructed. A white-haired old woman was waiting there. “When you fly, try not to look down,” the old woman said. Then she asked Mio to close her eyes.

Mio was frightened, so she trembled a little. But thanks to the old woman's words, she began to feel brave. When Mio said, “I want to fly,” the broom slowly rose into the air. She had finally become able to fly for the first time!

After that, Mio made a habit of practicing in the sky every day. She continued practicing little by little so that she could fly farther. One day, a strong wind caused the town clock to stop. Mio flew to the tall tower to repair it.

When Mio reached the tower, she found a blue bird inside the clock. Its wing had become trapped in the clock mechanism. Mio rescued the bird and brought it home. “Thank you,” the bird said, landing on Mio's shoulder.

That night, the bird said quietly, “Actually, I wrote that letter.” Mio was astonished. “Birds can write too?” she asked. The bird laughed and replied, “Of course. But I can't drive a broom.” They both laughed loudly, and the next morning they flew into the sky together again.
