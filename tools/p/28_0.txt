Title: Why the Jellyfish Has No Bones

Long ago, a magnificent Dragon King lived at the bottom of the sea. His castle had a large gate and was decorated with many beautiful seashells. One day, the queen became ill.

The king had called for the sea doctor. After examining the queen carefully, the doctor said, “We need a monkey's liver.” The king was troubled because there were no monkeys in the sea.

So the king summoned a jellyfish. In those days, jellyfish had strong bones and four legs. The king asked, “Please go to the land and bring back a monkey.” The jellyfish set off cheerfully.

The jellyfish left the sea and went into the forest. A monkey was sitting in a tree. The jellyfish tried asking, “Would you like to visit the Sea King's castle?” Thinking it sounded interesting, the monkey headed toward the sea with the jellyfish, bringing nothing along.

Before entering the sea, the jellyfish accidentally revealed an important secret. “The king wants your liver,” it said. The monkey was shocked but quickly thought of a plan. “I left my liver in the tree. I'll go and get it,” he said.

The jellyfish believed the story. It took the monkey back to the forest and waited near the sea. But the monkey stayed up in the tree and never came down. “No monkey leaves its liver in a tree!” he laughed.

The jellyfish returned to the castle without the monkey. When the king heard what had happened, he became furious. His servants removed all the jellyfish's bones. Without its bones, the jellyfish's body became soft.

From then on, the jellyfish could no longer walk on legs. Even today, it swims slowly through the sea with its soft, boneless body. Sometimes it looks toward the surface and wonders, “I wonder how that monkey is doing.” But it intends never to reveal another secret.
